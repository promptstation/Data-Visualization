# Domain Content Map — The IA Process: Research, Structure Design, Evaluation, and Iteration

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Execute the IA process across research (content-data inventories, audience-task research with analytic-question elicitation, precedent and constraint analysis), analysis (affinity and card-sorting organization studies, scheme selection, hierarchy modeling, priority curation), structure-labeling design (terminology development and testing, navigation architecture, wireframe-hierarchy specification, depth assignment, documentation packages), prototyping-validation (realistic prototypes, tree-first-click-task testing with persona-matched participants, findings-to-structure iteration), implementation support (handoff, integrity audits, label governance, telemetry setup), and lifecycle evolution (usage-driven refinement, change management with orientation support, periodic audits, rearchitecture triggers).
2. Apply genre-specific processes for dashboards, exploration workbenches, narrative reports, embedded-public, and mobile contexts.
3. Facilitate prioritization workshops and cross-discipline communication with decision-record documentation.
4. Select tools by fidelity-budget pragmatics.
5. Diagnose research-skipped, validation-skipped, documentation-absent, frozen, and over-process failures with calibrated repairs.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the professional process discipline: the IA process model — the workflow overview: the process phases (the research-and-inventory, the analysis-and-organization, the structure-and-labeling design, the prototyping-and-validation, the implementation-support-and-iteration — the double-diamond alignment: the problem-space exploration converging to structural requirements, the solution-space exploration converging to the architecture), the process-scaling discipline (the lightweight IA for single dashboards versus the full process for analytics platforms — the proportionate-rigor principle, the IA-effort-versus-rework-cost argument from the skipped-IA pitfall), and the IA-as-continuous-practice insight (the structure evolution with data-and-user changes — the periodic-rearchitecture triggers) as the process frame; the research-and-inventory phase — the discovery work: the content-and-data inventory (the dataset, metric, dimension, and view catalogs — the what-exists audit with the usage-and-value assessment, the content-audit methods adapted for data products: the freshness-quality-and-ownership annotation), the user-and-task research (the audience segmentation and persona development for analytics consumers — the role-goal-task matrices, the contextual-inquiry-and-interview methods — the how-people-actually-use-data discovery, the analytic-task elicitation: the questions-and-decisions inventory feeding the mantra-task mapping), the competitive-and-precedent analysis (the analogous-product structural review — the pattern-borrowing-with-critique discipline), and the technical-and-data-constraint mapping (the data-model-and-pipeline realities shaping possible structures — the granularity, freshness, and relationship constraints, the platform-and-tool limitations) as the discovery toolkit; the analysis-and-organization phase — the sensemaking work: the content-relationship analysis (the affinity-and-proximity mapping — the metric-and-view grouping by task, audience, and data relationship, the card-sorting methods for data structures: the open-and-closed card sorts for category validation with the analytics-specific adaptations: the metric-and-dimension sorting), the organization-scheme selection (the audience-based, task-based, object-based, and temporal schemes from the IA systems model — the hybrid-scheme-composition decisions), the hierarchy-and-depth modeling (the structure-diagram drafting — the view-tree-and-navigation-graph modeling, the breadth-depth optimization from the navigation discipline, the multiple-organization support — the same-content-different-paths via search, facets, and links), and the prioritization-and-curation (the importance ranking of content — the KPI-and-view-priority workshops with stakeholders, the cut-and-defer decisions — the minimum-viable-structure definition) as the analysis phase; the structure-and-labeling design phase — the architecture work: the labeling-system design (the terminology development — the metric-and-view naming with the user-vocabulary matching from the research, the label-consistency rules — the naming-convention documentation, the label testing — the comprehension-and-association verification), the navigation-system design (the global-local-contextual navigation architecture from section 7 — the menu-structure-and-wayfinding-system specification, the search-and-browse-balance decisions), the layout-and-wireframe specification (the zoning-and-template designs from section 4 — the page-and-dashboard wireframe systems with the hierarchy annotations from section 3, the responsive-behavior specification), the disclosure-and-depth specification (the layer-model assignment from section 6 — the what-lives-at-which-depth decisions, the mechanism selection per content type), and the IA documentation package (the structure diagrams, wireframes, label inventories, navigation maps, and decision-rationale records — the specification deliverables for implementation) as the design phase; the prototyping-and-validation phase — the evidence work: the IA prototyping methods (the clickable-wireframe-and-mockup prototypes for structural testing — the fidelity-appropriate-to-question discipline, the content-and-data-realistic prototyping — the real-metrics-and-labels-in-tests principle), the evaluation-method toolkit (the tree testing for structure-and-label validation — the task-based-path testing without visual-design confounds, the first-click testing for navigation-and-scent verification, the task-based usability testing on prototypes — the representative-question-completion assessment, the cognitive walkthroughs and expert reviews — the heuristic evaluation with IA-specific heuristics), the participant-and-recruitment practice (the audience-representative testing — the persona-matched recruitment, the sample-size pragmatics — the small-n iterative-testing traditions), and the findings-to-structure iteration (the test-result analysis — the path-efficiency-and-success-rate metrics, the structure-revision cycles — the iterate-until-tasks-succeed discipline) as the validation phase; the implementation-support phase — the realization work: the IA handoff practices (the developer-and-designer collaboration on structure — the specification-clarity-and-ambiguity resolution, the component-and-pattern mapping to design systems from section 12), the structure-integrity-during-implementation (the build-review checkpoints — the implemented-structure-versus-specification audits, the scope-creep-and-accumulation resistance — the layout-by-accumulation prevention from the pitfalls), the content-and-label-governance launch (the terminology-registry establishment, the label-and-copy QA passes), and the launch-measurement setup (the navigation-and-usage telemetry instrumentation from the analytics sections, the post-launch validation planning — the real-usage-versus-research-assumption comparison) as the realization phase; the iteration-and-evolution phase — the lifecycle work: the usage-driven refinement (the telemetry-analysis loops — the navigation-path-and-filter-usage patterns informing structure tuning, the search-log-and-support-ticket mining for gap identification), the change management for structures (the metric-and-view lifecycle changes — the addition-deprecation-migration patterns, the restructure-communication-and-orientation support — the what-changed guidance for users, the backward-compatibility-and-redirect patterns for deep links), the periodic-IA audits (the scheduled structure-health reviews — the consistency, findability, and coverage re-assessment, the debt-identification-and-remediation planning), and the scale-trigger recognition (the rearchitecture signals — the dataset growth, audience expansion, and task-complexity shifts demanding structural revision) as the lifecycle practice; the IA for specific data-product genres — the applied process: the dashboard-IA process (the metric-curation-and-panel-structure workflows — the KPI-prioritization sessions, the glanceability-driven zoning), the exploratory-analytics IA (the workspace-and-tool structure design — the flexible-structure provision within orientation support, the analysis-flow support patterns), the report-and-narrative IA (the linear-structure design — the section-flow-and-progressive-argument architectures from the narrative discipline, the scrollytelling structure integration), the embedded-and-public-data IA (the standalone-context constraints — the minimal-chrome-and-self-contained orientation, the SEO-and-discoverability considerations for public data), and the mobile-and-conversational IA (the constrained-structure adaptation — the priority curation and voice-query structures at awareness) as the genre applications; the collaboration-and-facilitation skills — the team layer: the IA workshop facilitation (the metric-prioritization-and-card-sorting-session running, the stakeholder-alignment techniques — the structure-decision-building consensus, the conflicting-priority negotiation — the evidence-and-task-anchored resolution), the cross-discipline communication (the IA-rationale explanation to engineers-and-executives — the structure-decision storytelling, the visual-specification communication — the wireframe-and-diagram literacy building), the documentation-and-knowledge sharing (the IA decision records and rationale libraries, the living-structure documentation maintenance), and the IA advocacy (the structural-quality championing in product teams — the skip-IA prevention from the pitfalls, the incremental-value demonstration for skeptical stakeholders) as the human-practice layer; the tools-and-methods ecosystem — the practitioner toolkit: the IA-and-wireframing tools (the diagramming-and-prototyping platforms — the structure-mapping-and-clickable-prototype tooling, the design-system-integrated wireframing), the evaluation tooling (the tree-testing-and-first-click platforms, the usability-testing infrastructure — the moderated-and-unmoderated options, the analytics-and-session-replay tools for navigation analysis), the research-and-inventory tools (the content-audit spreadsheets and catalogs, the card-sorting platforms), and the tool-selection pragmatics (the fidelity-and-budget matching — the lightweight-tools-for-lightweight-questions discipline) as the toolkit literacy; the IA-process failure modes — the diagnostic library: the research-skipped failure (the assumption-based structure — the designer-mental-model imposition, the stakeholder-opinion-as-user-evidence substitution), the validation-skipped failure (the untested-structure launch — the findability surprises at scale), the documentation-absent failure (the tribal-knowledge IA — the structure drift and inconsistency without specification), the frozen-structure failure (the launch-and-abandon IA — the evolution absence against changing data and needs), and the over-process failure (the bureaucracy paralysis — the full ceremony for small problems disproportion) with the calibrated repair patterns as the diagnostic discipline; and the IA-process deliverable — the research-to-iteration structural-design process capability with facilitation and tooling fluency.
