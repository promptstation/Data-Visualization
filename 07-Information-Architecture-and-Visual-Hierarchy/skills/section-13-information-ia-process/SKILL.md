# The IA Process: Research, Structure Design, Evaluation, and Iteration — Professional Training

## Mission

Produce professional-grade learning modules and training materials on **The IA Process: Research, Structure Design, Evaluation, and Iteration** within The art of organizing complex datasets into intuitive visual structures — Shneiderman's mantra, dashboard architecture, progressive disclosure, and navigation systems mastered as the structural backbone of data products.

Subject scope: Covers the professional process discipline: the IA process model — the workflow overview: the process phases (the research-and-inventory, the analysis-and-organization, the structure-and-labeling design, the prototyping-and-validation, the implementation-support-and-iteration — the double-diamond alignment: the problem-space exploration converging to structural requirements, the solution-space exploration converging to the architecture), the process-scaling discipline (the lightweight IA for single dashboards versus the full process for analytics platforms — the proportionate-rigor principle, the IA-effort-versus-rework-cost argument from the skipped-IA pitfall), and the IA-as-continuous-practice insight (the structure evolution with data-and-user changes — the periodic-rearchitecture triggers) as the process frame; the research-and-inventory phase — the discovery work: the content-and-data inventory (the dataset, metric, dimension, and view catalogs — the what-exists audit with the usage-and-value assessment, the content-audit methods adapted for data products: the freshness-quality-and-ownership annotation), the user-and-task research (the audience segmentation and persona development for analytics consumers — the role-goal-task matrices, the contextual-inquiry-and-interview methods — the how-people-actually-use-data discovery, the analytic-task elicitation: the questions-and-decisions inventory feeding the mantra-task mapping), the competitive-and-precedent analysis (the analogous-product structural review — the pattern-borrowing-with-critique discipline), and the technical-and-data-constraint mapping (the data-model-and-pipeline realities shaping possible structures — the granularity, freshness, and relationship constraints, the platform-and-tool limitations) as the discovery toolkit; the analysis-and-organization phase — the sensemaking work: the content-relationship analysis (the affinity-and-proximity mapping — the metric-and-view grouping by task, audience, and data relationship, the card-sorting methods for data structures: the open-and-closed card sorts for category validation with the analytics-specific adaptations: the metric-and-dimension sorting), the organization-scheme selection (the audience-based, task-based, object-based, and temporal schemes from the IA systems model — the hybrid-scheme-composition decisions), the hierarchy-and-depth modeling (the structure-diagram drafting — the view-tree-and-navigation-graph modeling, the breadth-depth optimization from the navigation discipline, the multiple-organization support — the same-content-different-paths via search, facets, and links), and the prioritization-and-curation (the importance ranking of content — the KPI-and-view-priority workshops with stakeholders, the cut-and-defer decisions — the minimum-viable-structure definition) as the analysis phase; the structure-and-labeling design phase — the architecture work: the labeling-system design (the terminology development — the metric-and-view naming with the user-vocabulary matching from the research, the label-consistency rules — the naming-convention documentation, the label testing — the comprehension-and-association verification), the navigation-system design (the global-local-contextual navigation architecture from section 7 — the menu-structure-and-wayfinding-system specification, the search-and-browse-balance decisions), the layout-and-wireframe specification (the zoning-and-template designs from section 4 — the page-and-dashboard wireframe systems with the hierarchy annotations from section 3, the responsive-behavior specification), the disclosure-and-depth specification (the layer-model assignment from section 6 — the what-lives-at-which-depth decisions, the mechanism selection per content type), and the IA documentation package (the structure diagrams, wireframes, label inventories, navigation maps, and decision-rationale records — the specification deliverables for implementation) as the design phase; the prototyping-and-validation phase — the evidence work: the IA prototyping methods (the clickable-wireframe-and-mockup prototypes for structural testing — the fidelity-appropriate-to-question discipline, the content-and-data-realistic prototyping — the real-metrics-and-labels-in-tests principle), the evaluation-method toolkit (the tree testing for structure-and-label validation — the task-based-path testing without visual-design confounds, the first-click testing for navigation-and-scent verification, the task-based usability testing on prototypes — the representative-question-completion assessment, the cognitive walkthroughs and expert reviews — the heuristic evaluation with IA-specific heuristics), the participant-and-recruitment practice (the audience-representative testing — the persona-matched recruitment, the sample-size pragmatics — the small-n iterative-testing traditions), and the findings-to-structure iteration (the test-result analysis — the path-efficiency-and-success-rate metrics, the structure-revision cycles — the iterate-until-tasks-succeed discipline) as the validation phase; the implementation-support phase — the realization work: the IA handoff practices (the developer-and-designer collaboration on structure — the specification-clarity-and-ambiguity resolution, the component-and-pattern mapping to design systems from section 12), the structure-integrity-during-implementation (the build-review checkpoints — the implemented-structure-versus-specification audits, the scope-creep-and-accumulation resistance — the layout-by-accumulation prevention from the pitfalls), the content-and-label-governance launch (the terminology-registry establishment, the label-and-copy QA passes), and the launch-measurement setup (the navigation-and-usage telemetry instrumentation from the analytics sections, the post-launch validation planning — the real-usage-versus-research-assumption comparison) as the realization phase; Write as an experienced practitioner, not as a summarizer of popular content. Every framework taught must be something a real team or professional could run: procedures they can execute, criteria they can judge with, and artifacts they can hand to a colleague. Do not present claims as settled when the field treats them as contested — the training must model evidence discipline.

## Core Output Requirements

- Deliverables are Markdown documents: the module, exercise sets with model solutions, and checklists. No placeholders, no "TODO" sections.
- Ground content in authoritative sources:
- Shneiderman's Visual Information-Seeking Mantra and the eight golden tasks of interface design for dynamic visual presentations, with the Plaisant lineage extensions
- The Rosenfeld-Arango information-architecture canon (organization, labeling, navigation, and search systems) applied to data products
- Few's dashboard design doctrine (Information Dashboard Design and Now You See It lineage) on glanceability, layout, and analytic arrangement
- The visual-hierarchy tradition from graphic design (the Lohr-lineage and Tufte layout craft, proximity-alignment-contrast principles) integrated with the Gestalt literature
- Nielsen Norman Group research on progressive disclosure, navigation, and analytics-UX usability findings
- The card-sorting and IA evaluation methods literature (tree testing, first-click studies, wayfinding research)
- Design-systems practice for data products (the enterprise dashboard-system and component-library documentation traditions)
- Maintain an evidence ledger while writing: every factual claim or number is either sourced, flagged as disputed/popular account, or omitted. Never invent statistics, studies, or citations.
- Distinguish established research findings from professional conventions and informed recommendations, and say which is which.
- Explain each specialized term in clear language on first use.

## Writing Standards

Throughout the material, prioritize language that is:

* Precise without becoming jargon-heavy
* Practical without discarding rigor
* Honest about limitations and contested findings without being defeatist
* Concrete — anchored in real cases, real artifacts, and verifiable numbers
* Progressive from fundamentals to expert judgment

Where a recommendation depends on context, explain the trade-off rather than presenting an absolute rule.

## Validation Gate

Before delivery, verify:

### Content
- all units present with correct depth for the scoped audience
- core distinctions established before they are used together
- every taught capability has a usable framework, not just an explanation

### Evidence
- every claim and number is sourced or explicitly flagged as disputed
- no invented statistics, studies, dates, or citations anywhere
- sources are authoritative; no SEO-farm or marketing claims presented as fact

### Capability
- each absorbed capability (5 in this section) is covered by teaching content AND at least one exercise with evaluation criteria
- exercises have model solutions in full-module mode

### Artifacts
- all gate checklists included and actionable as written
- template structure followed; no placeholder sections

### Quality
- trade-offs stated wherever recommendations are context-dependent
- terminology explained on first use
- reads as practitioner-written, not generic AI advice

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid: Everything-important — flat visual weight across all elements destroying hierarchy so nothing guides the eye; the anti-mantra dashboard requiring users to build their own overview from raw panels; Disclosure-by-burial — hiding information so deep in navigation layers that findability collapses; progressive disclosure executed without scent or wayfinding support; Layout-by-accumulation — dashboards grown panel-by-panel without an organizing scheme, where proximity relationships imply nonexistent groupings and the grid has died; Detail-without-context — drill-downs that teleport users to specifics with no orientation back to the whole, breaking the mantra's zoom-and-filter continuity and stranding users; IA-skipped development — building views before answering the organization and labeling questions, so structure decisions get frozen into code and every later fix costs a rewrite; exercises without evaluation criteria; modules that stop at inspiration without a single decision the learner can now make better; and any content that overstates certainty beyond what the evidence supports.

## Decision Heuristic

When allocating depth under time or length limits, ask:

1. Where will this audience actually stall in practice?
2. Which unit protects the most value if taught well?
3. Can the learner run the framework tomorrow without me?
4. Is every example doing work a plain sentence could not?
5. What would a skeptical domain expert say about this material?

If two topics compete for space, keep the one that changes a decision.

## Final Principle

The goal is not more content about IA and hierarchy. The goal is that a practitioner could take this material and perform: Execute the IA process across research (content-data inventories, audience-task research with analytic-question elicitation, precedent and constraint analysis), analysis (affinity and card-sorting organization studies, scheme selection, hierarchy modeling, priority curation), structure-labeling design (terminology development and testing, navigation architecture, wireframe-hierarchy specification, depth assignment, documentation packages), prototyping-validation (realistic prototypes, tree-first-click-task testing with persona-matched participants, findings-to-structure iteration), implementation support (handoff, integrity audits, label governance, telemetry setup), and lifecycle evolution (usage-driven refinement, change management with orientation support, periodic audits, rearchitecture triggers), diagnose research-skipped, validation-skipped, documentation-absent, frozen, and over-process failures with calibrated repairs — with the same evidence discipline the field's best practitioners use. The module must function simultaneously as a learning resource, a practical reference, and a working methodology.
