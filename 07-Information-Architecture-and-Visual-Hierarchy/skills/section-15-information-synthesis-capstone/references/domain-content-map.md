# Domain Content Map — Synthesis and Capstone: The Data Product Information Architecture

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Execute the complete data-product IA arc from context-requirements analysis through structure design, visual composition, system integration, evidence-based validation, and delivery-governance.
2. Demonstrate structural mastery through the mantra-load-hierarchy unifying frame applying the restraint principle and people-not-data organization across the consolidated foundations, mantra, hierarchy, layout, dashboard, disclosure, navigation, coordination, filtering, multiples, annotation.
3. Design-system, process, and pattern knowledge.
4. Integrate perception, load, rendering, spatial, latency, accessibility, math, EDA, embedding, narrative, semiographic, and executive disciplines into structural designs.
5. Prevent canonical failures (flat hierarchy, burial, accumulation, context-free detail, skipped IA, zombie systems, mazes, swamps) through documented prevention systems.
6. And uphold findability-equity, honest-structure, attention-respect, and trust-architecture ethics climbing toward structural leadership with organizational IA practice direction.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the consolidated capability map — the IA foundations (the four-systems model, the data-product challenges, the scent-and-wayfinding science, the load connection), the mantra mastery (the overview-zoom-filter-details-on-demand doctrine with the seven-task framework and golden-tasks coverage), the visual-hierarchy craft (the emphasis toolkit, tier systems, typography-and-color hierarchy, the audit-and-validation methods), the layout systems (grids, zoning, panels, density, responsive architecture), the dashboard architecture (glanceability, composition, metrics, filters, layers, suites, real-time systems), the progressive-disclosure systems (the layer model, mechanisms, adaptivity, trust disclosure), the navigation and wayfinding (movement taxonomies, orientation engineering, search integration, deep-linking, accessibility), the multi-view coordination (linking mechanisms, architectures, coherence, comparison patterns), the filter-and-query interfaces (dynamic queries, faceting, search, query builders, feedback design), the small-multiples-and-comparison systems (faceting mechanics, panel craft, arrangement families, selection frameworks), the annotation-and-explanation layers (function taxonomy, titles, labeling, callouts, narrative layers, governance), the design systems (tokens, components, patterns, encoding guidance, governance, theming), the IA process (research through iteration with facilitation and tools), and the pattern libraries (genre patterns, composite cases, anti-pattern anatomy, trend awareness) as one structural-design discipline; the capstone workflow — the master arc: context-and-requirements analysis (the audience-task-question elicitation, the data-and-constraint inventory, the genre-and-scale classification defining the structural approach), information-architecture design (the organization-scheme-and-hierarchy decisions, the navigation-and-wayfinding system, the disclosure-and-depth model, the labeling system — the complete structure specification with rationale), visual-structure design (the layout-zoning-and-grid system, the visual-hierarchy tier specification, the dashboard-and-view compositions, the comparison-and-coordination architectures, the annotation-and-explanation layers), system integration (the design-system alignment — the component-and-token conformance, the multi-view-and-filter coordination specification, the responsive-and-accessible structure adaptation), validation-and-refinement (the tree-first-click-and-task-testing execution, the hierarchy-and-glanceability audits, the wayfinding-and-scent verification, the cognitive-load assessment — the evidence-based structure iteration), and delivery-and-governance (the implementation support and integrity audits, the telemetry-and-iteration setup, the documentation-and-decision records, the evolution-and-lifecycle planning) as the complete structural-design cycle; the integration thesis — the structural-mastery judgment: IA as the invisible success factor (the structure users never notice when right and cannot ignore when wrong — the findability-and-orientation as precondition for all visualization value, the mantra-load-hierarchy triad as the unifying frame: the task doctrine, the cognitive budget, and the perceptual signaling working as one system), the restraint principle (the simplest structure serving the tasks — the depth-breadth-and-disclosure economy, the accumulation-resistance discipline against the everything-important collapse), and the structure-serves-people-not-data insight (the user-question organization over database-schema mirroring — the mental-model-matching imperative) as the mature structural judgment; cross-course integration — the curriculum position: Gestalt and preattentive science (courses 1-2) as the perceptual foundation for hierarchy-and-grouping decisions, cognitive load (course 3) as the budget discipline bounding structure complexity, graphics APIs and D3 (courses 4-5) as the rendering-implementation layers for structures, spatial and 3D (course 6) as the genre application of navigation-and-coordination patterns, latency tuning (course 8) as the responsiveness floor for dynamic queries and coordination, accessibility (course 9) as the inclusion obligation for navigation, disclosure, and annotation, linear algebra and EDA (courses 10-11) as the data understanding feeding structure decisions, dimensionality reduction (course 12) as the high-dimensional-structure display partner, scrollytelling (course 13) as the narrative-structure genre, semiographics (course 14) as the symbol-and-metaphor layer in labeling and annotation, and executive communication (course 15) as the high-stakes audience context for glanceable structure; case synthesis — the worked systems (the enterprise-analytics-platform IA — the multi-audience suite with dashboard, exploration, and report genres integrated, the operational-monitoring product — the real-time-alert-investigate structure with wall-and-workbench patterns, the public-health-data portal — the narrative-exploration hybrid with accessibility-and-trust architecture, the embedded-analytics suite — the contextual product-integrated structures with deep-link architectures) decomposed through the capstone workflow; failure post-mortems — the canonical errors (the everything-important flat hierarchy, the disclosure-by-burial, the layout-by-accumulation, the detail-without-context, the skipped-IA development, the zombie design system, the maze navigation, the filter swamp, the coordination invisibility, the stale-annotation trust collapse) with the prevention systems from sections 1-14; the maturity model — stage one (the single-dashboard structure — the hierarchy-and-layout craft applied), stage two (the multi-view-product IA — the navigation, disclosure, and coordination systems), stage three (the platform-and-suite architecture — the design-system governance and process mastery), stage four (the structural leadership — the organizational-IA-practice direction, the pattern-library stewardship, the cross-discipline structural strategy) as the capability roadmap; the structural-ethics position — the standard: the findability-equity obligation (the structure serving all audiences and abilities — the accessible navigation and disclosure as architecture not accommodation), the honest-structure commitment (the labeling-and-hierarchy truthfulness — the emphasis matching genuine importance not political priority, the dark-pattern avoidance in navigation and disclosure — the no-burial-of-unwelcome-information discipline), the attention-respect principle (the cognitive-budget stewardship — the load-minimizing structures honoring user time, the choice-architecture responsibility — the defaults-and-priority ethics), and the trust-architecture duty (the provenance, freshness, and definition access as structural citizens — the transparency by design) as the structural ethics; and the capstone — a complete data-product information architecture: select a real analytics context with multi-dataset multi-audience complexity, run the research-and-inventory phase (audience-task elicitation, data-and-constraint mapping), design the complete structure (organization, hierarchy, navigation, disclosure, labeling specification with rationale records), compose the visual structure (layout zoning, hierarchy tiers, dashboard-and-view compositions, coordination-and-annotation systems), integrate with design-system-and-accessibility standards, validate through tree-first-click-task testing and hierarchy audits iterating on evidence, and deliver the specification package with implementation-support plan, telemetry-and-iteration setup, and governance documentation.
