## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Execute the complete responsiveness-engineering arc from baseline-diagnosis through architecture-design, implementation, validation, and institutionalization.
2. Demonstrate latency-as-system-property judgment applying dominant-stage targeting, evidence hierarchy (field-percentiles over lab-averages over opinions), and equity-honesty balance across the consolidated perception-pipeline-measurement-input-frame-render-data-motion-scale-device-architecture-governance knowledge.
3. Integrate Gestalt-motion, load, graphics, D3, spatial, IA, accessibility, math, EDA, embedding, narrative, semiographic, and executive disciplines into responsive systems.
4. Prevent canonical failures (unmeasured optimization, cargo-cult throttling, average illusions, monoliths, perceived neglect, thrash-explosion, over-fetch-waterfall, blank-wait-fake-progress, flagship-assumption, governance-vacuum) through documented prevention systems.
5. And uphold performance-equity, perception-honesty, resource-respect, and trust-through-responsiveness ethics climbing toward performance-leadership with governance institution-building and predictive architecture direction.
