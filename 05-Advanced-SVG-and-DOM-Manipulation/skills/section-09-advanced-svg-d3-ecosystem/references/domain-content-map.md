## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Maps the surrounding ecosystem: the modular-D3 architecture — the composition reality: the independent-module design (the d3 umbrella package versus per-module imports — the d3-selection, d3-scale, d3-shape selective adoption, the tree-shaking and bundle-size benefits, the version-compatibility matrix across modules), the module-dependency graph awareness (the internal cross-dependencies — the d3-scale on d3-interpolate chains), and the non-DOM module independence (the d3-array, d3-geo, d3-delaunay usability without any DOM — the server-side and worker computation patterns) as the modular literacy; Observable Plot — the higher-level sibling: the Plot concept (the mark-and-encode declarative grammar built atop D3 — the plot specification objects producing SVG with automatic scales, axes, and legends), the mark vocabulary (the dot-line-area-bar-rect-cell-rule-text-tick-frame marks with the transform family — the bin-group-stack-sort-dodge operations), the Plot-D3 relationship (the escape-hatch patterns — the post-hoc D3 manipulation of Plot output, the custom-mark authoring, when to descend to raw D3) and the Plot-fit assessment (the exploratory-and-standard-chart velocity versus the bespoke-interaction limits) as the grammar-tier mastery; the Vega ecosystem position — the specification tier: the Vega and Vega-Lite grammar-of-graphics specifications (the JSON-declarative visualization language — the interactive-signal system), the Vega-Embed rendering integration, and the ecosystem-positioning comparison (the Vega-spec shareability and tooling versus the D3-code flexibility versus the Plot middle ground) as the declarative-alternative literacy; the community-library landscape — the built-on-D3 tier: the React-ecosystem libraries (the visx low-level-primitive approach, the Nivo configurable-chart tier, the Recharts component-charts — the abstraction-spectrum placement), the general charting libraries with D3 internals (the ECharts-Chart.js-Plotly backend awareness), and the specialized-domain libraries (the topojson-client, the d3-annotation, the d3-legend ecosystem extensions) as the consumer-and-contributor map; the Observable platform literacy — the notebook environment: the Observable-notebook mechanics (the reactive-cell model — the variable-dependency re-evaluation, the md-FileTree-HTML built-ins), the notebook-as-development-environment workflow (the prototyping-to-production extraction path — the module-export and bundling of notebook code), the Observable Framework static-site generator (the data-dashboard publishing pipeline — the loaders and data-files architecture), and the community-notebook mining practice (the Bostock-and-community exemplar library as the pattern source — the search-and-adapt discipline with attribution norms) as the platform fluency; the data-loading and format ecosystem — the ingestion tier: the d3-dsv-json-fetch modules (the autoType parsing conveniences and their hazards — the implicit-conversion pitfalls), the format-ecosystem integration (the Apache-Arrow and parquet-wasm columnar ingestion from the graphics-data discipline, the TopoJSON geographic pipelines, the ndjson streaming patterns), and the API-and-CMS source patterns (the fetch-orchestration, the caching and revalidation strategies for visualization data) as the ingestion literacy; the TypeScript-with-D3 practice — the typed development: the @types/d3 ecosystem (the per-module type definitions, the generic-typed selections — the Selection< BaseType, DatumType, ParentType, ExtraType> four-generic discipline), the typed-datum patterns (the interface-per-visualization-datum conventions, the scale-and-accessor type inference), and the typed-D3 friction points (the event-typing patterns, the transition-and-join type complexities) as the production-typing craft; the build-and-bundle integration — the delivery tier: the bundler handling of D3 modules (the ESM-native imports through Vite-webpack-esbuild, the CJS-legacy considerations for older toolchains), the bundle-optimization practice (the per-module imports reducing payload, the dynamic-import code-splitting for heavy modules like d3-geo), and the CDN-versus-bundled decisions (the script-tag prototyping versus production bundling) as the delivery engineering; the version-evolution management — the lifecycle literacy: the D3-version-history awareness (the v3-to-v4 API revolution — the join-and-module changes, the v4-to-v7 evolutionary stability, the legacy-tutorial-hazard — the pre-v4 patterns pervading old documentation and Stack Overflow requiring the version-dating discipline for all referenced examples), the upgrade-migration practice (the codemod-and-manual patterns for major bumps), and the deprecation-tracking (the SMIL and legacy-API sunset patterns) as the maintenance literacy; the contribution-and-extension pathways — the participation tier: the module-source navigation (the small-focused-module readability — the d3-scale internals study as the deep-mastery path), the plugin-and-extension authoring (the custom-scale, interpolator, and layout authoring patterns), and the issue-and-PR participation norms in the D3 repositories as the ecosystem-citizenship layer; the ecosystem-selection framework — the decision synthesis: the requirement-to-tier mapping (the standard-exploratory charts to Plot-VegaLite, the custom-bespoke-interactive to raw D3, the framework-native-component needs to visx-Nivo tiers, the extreme-scale to canvas-WebGL descent per the graphics discipline) with the hybrid-stacking patterns (the Plot-for-exploration D3-for-production workflow, the D3-computation with framework-rendering split from section 10) as the selection discipline; and the ecosystem deliverable — the module, grammar-tier, community-library, platform, and tooling fluency for informed selection and deep mastery.
