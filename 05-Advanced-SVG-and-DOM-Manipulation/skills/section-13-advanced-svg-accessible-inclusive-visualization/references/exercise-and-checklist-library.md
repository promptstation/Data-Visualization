# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Build accessible SVG through role-title-desc semantics
**Scenario:** provide a realistic D3 and SVG scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** build accessible SVG through role-title-desc semantics, graphics-module roles, focusability, and DOM-order reading discipline, author text alternatives on the summary-to-detailed spectrum answering what-data-shows with human-authored meaning, provide data-table and download equivalents through shared-source synchronization, enforce color inclusion (contrast ratios, CVD palettes, redundant encoding, dark-print parity) and keyboard interaction parity (roving tabindex, value announcements, hover-focus equivalence, dynamic focus management), gate motion through reduced-motion integration and flash safety.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.
