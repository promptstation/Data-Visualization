# Domain Content Map — Validating Salience in Practice: Testing, Telemetry, and Evidence Loops

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Operate the four-tier salience validation stack (lab calibration, rapid tests, field A/B, production telemetry) matched to decision stakes.
2. Design seeded-anomaly protocols and hypothesis-driven emphasis experiments with within-subjects power discipline, specialize eye-tracking metrics (time-to-first-fixation, dwell, distractor ratios) beyond heatmap theater, instrument privacy-compliant telemetry with longitudinal decay monitoring, document decision-evidence records and benchmark libraries.
3. Communicate salience evidence to stakeholders resolving contested emphasis through data.
4. Run continuous validation cadences from design-time through periodic re-benchmarking.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Consolidates the measurement practice into a professional evidence system: the salience-validation stack — the layered evidence architecture: the lab-tier (the controlled search and singleton-detection experiments from section 7 — the channel-parameter calibration), the rapid-tier (the five-second tests, first-fixation checks, and hallway protocols from the course-1 validation practice specialized to emphasis questions), the field-tier (the A/B variant testing on production dashboards — the task-completion and detection-rate comparisons), and the telemetry-tier (the behavioral analytics from live usage — the click, hover, acknowledge, and dwell responses to emphasis elements) as the four-tier evidence pyramid with the effort-to-stakes matching discipline; the hypothesis-driven salience testing — the experiment design: the emphasis-question formulation (does the highlighted element get found faster? does the alert get acknowledged sooner? does the intended first-fixation land?) converting design intentions into testable predictions with the null-and-alternative discipline (the course-19 statistics applied — the power considerations for the typically-small participant counts of design research, the effect-size reporting over p-value fixation); the seeded-anomaly protocol — the workhorse test: the anomaly-injection methodology (the synthetic exceptions planted in realistic displays at controlled salience levels — the detection-time, miss-rate, and false-alarm measurement across design variants), the realism-calibration (the participant-familiarity and display-density matching to production conditions), and the variant-comparison structure (the within-subjects counterbalanced designs maximizing power per participant) as the dashboard-emphasis gold-standard test; the eye-tracking specialization — the gaze evidence: the salience-specific gaze metrics (the time-to-first-fixation on the emphasis target — the capture-speed measure, the fixation-dwell on highlighted elements — the engagement verification, the distractor-fixation ratio — the competition measure, the scanpath-to-target efficiency) with the heatmap-honesty discipline (the aggregate heatmaps as communication artifacts not analysis — the AOI-statistics as the evidence, the course-1 section-13 method specialized); the telemetry instrumentation — the production evidence: the emphasis-response tracking (the alert-acknowledgment latency distributions, the highlight-click rates, the filter-and-drill sequences following emphasis — the revealed-attention data), the longitudinal-decay monitoring (the response-rate drift over weeks — the habituation detection from section 12 operationalized), and the privacy-compliant instrumentation (the behavioral-telemetry consent and aggregation boundaries — the governance layer) as the always-on evidence stream; the saliency-model-assisted review — the computational tier: the prediction-overlay audits (the section-8 model heatmaps compared against intended hierarchies as the pre-user-test screening), the automated-composition checks (the contrast-ratio verification, the salience-budget counting — the loud-element inventory automation, the accidental-hotspot detection) integrating model predictions into the design-review pipeline with the human-judgment-final principle; the evidence-documentation practice — the institutional memory: the salience-decision records (the tested variants, the measured outcomes, the chosen design with rationale — the decision-evidence log), the benchmark library (the accumulated internal reference values — your dashboards' typical detection times and acknowledgment latencies enabling drift detection), and the failure-case archive (the deployed emphasis failures with root-cause tags — the cry-wolf incidents, the missed-critical-alert post-mortems) as the compounding evidence assets; the stakeholder-evidence communication — the persuasion layer: the salience-evidence presentation (the before-after search-time comparisons, the animation-of-gaze-path demonstrations, the telemetry-trend exhibits — the executive-legible proof formats), the contested-emphasis negotiation (the data-driven resolution of taste disputes — 'the CEO wants the logo bigger' resolved through first-fixation evidence and task-performance data), and the evidence-proportionality messaging (the honest uncertainty communication — the small-sample caveats and effect-size ranges) making salience science organizationally actionable; the validation anti-patterns — the methodology failures: the heatmap-theater (the pretty gaze plots without hypotheses or statistics), the n-of-one-validation (the single-stakeholder glance as evidence — the designer-blindness trap), the lab-field-gap neglect (the pristine-search-task results assumed to transfer to the messy production dashboard), and the telemetry-without-baseline (the response-rate numbers without the pre-change comparison — the uninterpretable metric) with the corrections embedded in the protocol designs; the continuous-validation operating model — the cadence system: the design-time validation (the tier-matched testing per new emphasis feature), the deploy-time monitoring (the telemetry baselines and alert thresholds for emphasis performance), the periodic re-benchmarking (the quarterly salience-effect re-measurement catching decay and drift), and the annual-evidence-review (the benchmark-library and failure-archive synthesis informing standards updates) as the sustainable evidence operations; and the validation deliverable — the four-tier evidence system with documentation, communication, and continuous-operation discipline.
