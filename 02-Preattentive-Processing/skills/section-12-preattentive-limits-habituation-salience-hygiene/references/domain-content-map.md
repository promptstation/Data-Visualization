## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Manage preattentive limits through conjunction workarounds (hierarchical emphasis, spatial segregation, interactive pre-filtering), distractor-uniformity restoration, and density-gated level-of-detail salience, counter habituation with change-only signaling, variable schedules, escalation ladders, and periodic re-baselining, govern finite salience budgets (one-to-three focal points, allocation triage, restoration audits) with alert false-positive tuning against cry-wolf dynamics, prevent pop-out inversions (distraction capture, aesthetic conflict, expert filtering).
2. Detect decay through acknowledgment-latency telemetry and re-benchmarking.
