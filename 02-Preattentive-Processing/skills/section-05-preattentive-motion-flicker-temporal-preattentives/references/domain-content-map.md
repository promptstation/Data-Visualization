# Domain Content Map — Motion, Flicker, and Temporal Preattentives: The Urgency Channels

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Deploy motion as the dominant urgency channel through onset-offset transient engineering with direction-speed-coherence mechanism knowledge.
2. Manage flicker within photosensitive-safety limits (WCAG flash thresholds) and discomfort constraints as licensed-only emphasis, counter change-blindness through transient-carrying updates with animation-speed grammar (100-300ms comprehension windows, natural easing).
3. Design streaming displays (scroll conventions, flash highlights, rate aggregation) against motion-habituation decay, and enforce reduced-motion fallbacks preserving salience through luminance-size channels preventing decorative, competing, silent-jump, and seizure-risk failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the time-domain salience family: the motion-channel primacy — the dominance finding: motion as the strongest attention-capture channel (the documented motion-onset priority — new motion detected faster and more reliably than any static feature difference, the peripheral-motion sensitivity — the magnocellular pathway's motion specialization with wide-field low-resolution coverage), making motion the emergency channel of the visual system and the highest-power highest-risk design tool; the motion-feature channels — the mechanism decomposition: the direction-selective processing (the Reichardt-detector and motion-energy model lineage at awareness depth, the direction-specific aftereffects proving channel structure), the speed-sensitivity bands, and the coherent-motion detection (the global-motion binding — the course-1 common-fate law as the motion-grouping complement) as the motion-channel architecture; onset and offset — the transient channels: the documented appearance-disappearance capture superiority (the abrupt-onset priority findings — new objects outranking feature singletons, the offset-capture asymmetry), the transient-based change detection (the flicker-paradigm findings quantifying change sensitivity), and the design applications (the enter-exit animations as onset-engineered notifications, the flash-highlight conventions for updated values in live displays, the new-element introduction choreography); flicker — the double-edged channel: the documented flicker sensitivity (the critical-flicker-frequency findings, the temporal-contrast detection thresholds) with the capture applications (the pulsing-alert patterns, the blink-rate salience hierarchy) and the hard safety limits (the photosensitive-seizure thresholds — the WCAG three-flash limits as the non-negotiable constraint, the discomfort and fatigue findings at sub-seizure rates, the vestibular-trigger adjacencies — the course-1 motion-safety integration) making flicker a licensed-only tool; motion-versus-static competition — the mixed-field dynamics: the documented findings on motion singletons among static distractors (the automatic capture — motion winning even when task-irrelevant, the capture-cost: the irrelevant-motion degradation of the primary task) with the design translation (any persistent motion in a display taxes every other task — the ambient-motion budget discipline); the change-blindness counterpoint — the failure mode: the documented change-blindness phenomena (large changes missed without local motion transients — the flicker and mudsplash demonstrations, the saccade-suppression blindness) establishing the rule: changes without transients go unseen, changes with transients cannot be ignored — the transient-design decision for every update; the animation-speed grammar — the temporal craft: the duration conventions (the documented perceptual thresholds — under-100ms reading as instant, the 100-300ms transition-comprehension window, the over-500ms sluggishness perception), the easing and trajectory naturalness (the physical-plausibility findings — the biomechanical motion preferences), and the speed-salience relationship (faster motion capturing more strongly — the velocity-salience curve) as the timing-parameters toolkit; the motion-habituation dynamic — the decay reality: the documented adaptation of motion capture (the repeated-motion desensitization, the persistent-animation invisibility after minutes — the habituation timeline findings) with the countermeasure patterns (the variable-interval motion, the motion-on-change-only discipline, the periodic-reset strategies) previewing section 12's fatigue depth; the streaming-data application — the live-display craft: the real-time chart update patterns (the scroll-and-shift conventions maintaining continuity — the motion-parallax of time-series streaming, the value-change flash highlighting, the threshold-crossing animation triggers) with the update-rate design (the frame-rate versus data-rate distinction, the aggregation-window smoothing preventing flicker-flood) as the temporal-dashboard skill; the reduced-motion obligation — the accessibility contract: the prefers-reduced-motion design requirement (the static and fade-only fallback modes — the WCAG animation criteria, the vestibular-disorder accommodation) with the salience-preservation challenge (the non-motion emphasis alternatives — the luminance and size channels carrying emphasis for motion-sensitive users) as the inclusive-motion design; the motion anti-patterns — the failure catalog: the decorative-perpetual-motion (the ambient loops taxing attention with zero information — the capture-waste), the multi-motion competition (several simultaneous animations — the bottleneck overload from section 2's attentional-blink), the motion-without-transient (the silent-jump updates defeating change detection — the change-blindness trap), and the seizure-risk violation (the high-frequency large-area flash — the safety-line crossing) as the temporal-hygiene rules; and the temporal-channel deliverable — the motion-onset-flicker salience system with safety limits, habituation counters, and reduced-motion equity.
