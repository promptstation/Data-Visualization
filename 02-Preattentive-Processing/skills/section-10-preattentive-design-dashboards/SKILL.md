# Preattentive Design for Dashboards: Emphasis, Alerts, and Glanceability — Professional Training

## Mission

Produce professional-grade learning modules and training materials on **preattentive Design for Dashboards: Emphasis, Alerts, and Glanceability** within The science of visual attributes (color hue, size, orientation, motion) processed in milliseconds before conscious attention — engineered to direct the viewer's eye instantly in data visualization.

Subject scope: Applies the science to the dashboard craft: the glanceability concept — the dashboard ideal: the single-fixation comprehension target (the documented glanceable-design research — what can be extracted in under-250ms: the gross state, the presence of exceptions, the dominant trend direction) defining the preattentive layer's job — the state-at-a-glance readout before any deliberate inspection; the emphasis-hierarchy architecture — the tiered salience plan: the three-tier emphasis system (tier one: the decision-critical exception signals using the strongest channels — motion-onset, high-luminance-contrast, isolated position; tier two: the status-overview structure using mid-strength channels — color families, size gradations, region containment; tier three: the reference detail at baseline salience — quiet, uniform, receding) with the channel-allocation per tier as the salience-budget plan; the alert-design system — the exception-signaling craft: the alert-channel selection (the criticality-to-channel mapping — the life-critical versus informational tiers using motion-and-luminance versus color-only signaling, the audible-visual pairing for the unattended displays), the alert-presentation patterns (the in-place highlight versus banner-intrusion versus dedicated-alert-panel trade-offs — the capture-versus-context spectrum, the annotation-linked alert connecting signal to explanation), and the alert-state lifecycle (the new-acknowledged-resolved visual states preventing re-capture of handled alerts — the alert-fatigue mitigation); the KPI-and-number emphasis — the scalar-display craft: the big-number dashboard patterns (the KPI-tile salience design — the size-and-weight hierarchy across tiles, the change-indicator channels — the arrow-color-sparkline combinations, the threshold-crossing state changes — the conditional-formatting as applied preattentive encoding) with the number-comparison support (the aligned-decimal and tabular-figure conventions enabling the preattentive length-parsing, the reference-line and target-marker emphasis); the status-overview patterns — the field-display craft: the heatmap-and-status-grid glanceability (the color-field anomaly visibility — the single-off-color-cell pop in uniform grids, the density limits where cell-level pop fails and region-level structure takes over), the traffic-light conventions (the red-amber-green cultural-channel double-duty with the CVD-redundancy obligation — the shape-or-icon companions), and the small-multiples monitoring walls (the cross-panel exception scan — the identical-form violation detection, the panel-order and highlight-consistency requirements); the map-and-spatial dashboards — the geographic layer: the choropleth and symbol-map salience (the regional-color-cluster reading as preattentive pattern, the outlier-region pop, the map-specific pitfalls — the area-size salience confound: large regions dominating regardless of value, the classification-scheme effects on pattern salience) with the spatial-exception signaling (the cluster-hotspot highlighting, the animation for emerging spatial patterns); the multi-screen and control-room context — the ambient-display tier: the wall-display design (the viewing-distance channel selection — the size, luminance, and motion channels surviving distance where color-hue and form detail fail, the ambient-peripheral design from section 9's divided-attention mode), the multi-monitor salience coordination (the cross-screen attention routing — the beacon patterns drawing gaze between screens), and the lighting-environment calibration (the control-room dim versus office bright contrast-ratio adjustments) as the environment-hardened salience design; the interactive emphasis layer — the dynamic salience: the hover-and-selection feedback (the immediate-state-change as the responsiveness signal — the under-100ms feedback rule connecting to the latency course 8, the highlight-and-dim interaction patterns), the filter-and-drill salience updates (the re-computation of emphasis after interaction — the context-changed re-salience), and the cross-filter-linked highlighting (the coordinated-view emphasis propagation — the course-1 common-fate dynamics applied to interaction) as the responsive-salience system; the glanceability testing — the validation practice: the five-second-and-shorter tests (the state-extraction protocols — what did you see? anything wrong? which panel matters most?), the exception-detection timed trials (the seeded-anomaly find-time measurement across design variants), and the alert-fatigue longitudinal observation (the acknowledged-ignoring rates in production) as the dashboard-salience evidence system; the dashboard anti-patterns — the failure catalog: the everything-critical dashboard (uniform loud styling — the salience-flatline), the alert-storm design (every threshold firing motion alerts — the bottleneck saturation), the decoration-competition (branding and imagery out-salienting data — the chrome-flip), and the glance-oracle gap (designs requiring reading to know if attention is needed — the state-invisibility failure) as the hygiene rules; and the dashboard deliverable — the tiered, tested, fatigue-resistant emphasis system.

Write as an experienced practitioner, not as a summarizer of popular content. ## Anti-Patterns

Avoid: Salience inflation — highlighting everything until nothing pops; preattentive power is relative and budget-limited, diluted by overuse (the cry-wolf effect on the salience map); Confusing salience with meaning — a popped element directs the eye but carries no interpretation; preattentive capture without deliberate-support structure leaves viewers looking at the right place without understanding; Conjunction blindness — expecting combinations of features (red AND large AND tilted) to pop when only single features do, producing serial-search tasks mislabeled as glances; Habituation neglect — persistent highlights and badges losing capture power through neural adaptation, the alert-fatigue dynamic killing always-on emphasis; Accessibility-assumed pop — preattentive effects varying with color vision (hue channels degraded for CVD viewers) and age, single-channel salience failing for part of the audience; exercises without evaluation criteria; modules that stop at inspiration without a single decision the learner can now make better; and any content that overstates certainty beyond what the evidence supports.

## Decision Heuristic

When allocating depth under time or length limits, ask:

1. Where will this audience actually stall in practice?
2. Which unit protects the most value if taught well?
3. Can the learner run the framework tomorrow without me?
4. Is every example doing work a plain sentence could not?
5. What would a skeptical domain expert say about this material?

If two topics compete for space, keep the one that changes a decision.
