## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Engineer spatial salience through position hierarchies (scan-pattern primacies, critical-signal zones) with eccentricity-gradient channel matching (motion-luminance for peripheral reach, form-color for foveal detail), deploy depth-ordering (occlusion, shadow, elevation), containment (region singletons, tint-weight grammar), alignment pathways, and isolation whitespace as emphasis channels.
2. Manage clutter as salience denominator with decluttering amplification and cluster-context grouping effects, compose multi-channel spatial emphasis under congruence and budget discipline.
3. Validate through first-fixation analysis and timed find-tests preventing corner-burial, clutter-competition, and false-affordance failures.
