# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Execute the complete salience-engineering cycle from task-audience analysis through channel-allocation specification
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Preattentive processing scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** execute the complete salience-engineering cycle from task-audience analysis through channel-allocation specification, tiered composition, limits-habituation audit, and validated delivery with documentation, applying the salience-as-service principle allocating capture by cost-of-missing rather than loudness, integrating grouping, load, rendering, latency, accessibility, EDA, narrative, and executive-display disciplines around the preattentive foundation, preventing canonical failures (inflation, conjunction mislabeling, fatigue blindness, CVD fragility, silent jumps, validation theater) through budget-hygiene systems, and upholding measured, honest, equitable, sustainable salience ethics climbing toward team-level salience governance.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.
