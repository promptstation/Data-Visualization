Execute the complete salience-engineering cycle from task-audience analysis through channel-allocation specification, tiered composition, limits-habituation audit, and validated delivery with documentation, applying the salience-as-service principle allocating capture by cost-of-missing rather than loudness, integrating grouping, load, rendering, latency, accessibility, EDA, narrative, and executive-display disciplines around the preattentive foundation, preventing canonical failures (inflation, conjunction mislabeling, fatigue blindness, CVD fragility, silent jumps, validation theater) through budget-hygiene systems, and upholding measured, honest, equitable, sustainable salience ethics climbing toward team-level salience governance.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the consolidated framework — the millisecond-visual-system foundations (the two-stage architecture, the parallel-channel processing), the mechanism model (feature maps, salience maps, guided search, contrast relativity), the channel families with strength hierarchies (luminance-hue-saturation color, size-orientation-shape-length form, motion-onset-flicker temporal, position-depth-enclosure-whitespace spatial), the measurement science (search slopes, singleton thresholds, signal detection, gaze metrics), the top-down determinants (task sets, expertise, expectation, vigilance), the application systems (dashboard emphasis tiers, alert design, glanceability, data-encoding channel selection), the limits and hygiene (conjunctions, distractors, density, habituation, budgets, cry-wolf dynamics), the streaming dynamics (transients, continuity, rate adaptation), and the validation stack as one salience-engineering discipline; the capstone workflow — the master arc: task-and-audience analysis (the viewer's questions and modes — monitoring, analysis, lookup; the expertise mix and accessibility requirements), the salience-specification (the intended attention hierarchy — what must pop first, second, never; the channel-allocation plan from the strength tables with redundancy for CVD), the composition execution (the quiet-canvas construction, the tier-one-two-three emphasis build, the encoding-channel selection per data type), the limits-audit (the conjunction, distractor, density, and budget checks plus the habituation-decay plan for persistent elements), the validation (the tier-matched evidence — seeded-anomaly tests, first-fixation verification, telemetry baselines), and the documentation (the salience-spec record with cue-channel rationale and evidence) as the complete professional cycle; the salience-as-service principle — the integration insight: preattentive channels as the display's steering system serving the viewer's decision tasks (the normative-salience discipline from section 8 — decision-relevance setting the hierarchy, capture-power allocated not by data loudness or designer taste but by the cost of missing each signal), making salience engineering an act of respect for the viewer's finite attention; cross-course integration — the curriculum position: Gestalt grouping (course 1) as the structural binding that salience rides on (the quiet-canvas and grouping-hierarchy preconditions), cognitive load (course 3) as the capacity economics salience serves (the attention-budget connection), the rendering APIs (courses 4-5) as the implementation layer where channel decisions become shader and style properties, the latency discipline (course 8) as the transient and feedback timing constraint, accessibility (course 9) as the redundant-channel mandate, EDA (course 11) as the data-truth check before salience-trust, scrollytelling (course 13) as the narrative-directed attention sequence, and executive display (course 15) as the glanceability-stakes context; case synthesis — the worked exemplars (the operations-monitoring wall redesign from everything-loud to tiered-salience with detection-time evidence, the trading-dashboard transient-grammar overhaul defeating alert fatigue, the public-health live dashboard balancing glanceability and accessibility, the analytics-tool emphasis system adapting to expert-novice divergence) decomposed through the capstone workflow; failure post-mortems — the canonical errors (salience inflation and the flatline dashboard, conjunction mislabeling as glance design, habituation-blind persistent alerts, single-channel CVD-fragile emphasis, silent-jump streaming, heatmap-theater validation) with the prevention systems from sections 12-14; the maturity model — stage one (channel literacy — seeing salience structure in displays), stage two (deliberate channel-allocation in own compositions), stage three (validated salience systems with testing and telemetry fluency), stage four (salience-governance — budgets, hygiene cadences, and standards for teams and products) as the capability roadmap; the evidence-and-ethics position — the professional standard: measured-not-assumed capture (the search-tested emphasis), honest salience (emphasis serving truth-finding not conclusion-steering — the section-8 ethics), equitable channels (redundancy for every viewer — the accessibility default), and sustainable budgets (the fatigue-resistant design respecting attention as the viewer's property) as the salience-engineering ethics; and the capstone — a complete salience-engineered display system: select a real-time or dense-data display context, run the task-audience-mode analysis, specify the attention hierarchy with channel-allocation and redundancy plans, execute the composition with quiet-canvas and tiered emphasis, run the limits and habituation audit, validate through seeded-anomaly tests and first-fixation evidence with telemetry baselines, and deliver the system with salience-spec documentation and governance recommendations.
