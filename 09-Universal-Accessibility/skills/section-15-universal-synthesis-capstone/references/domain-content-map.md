# Domain Content Map — Synthesis and Capstone: The Universally Accessible Visualization System

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Execute the complete inclusion-engineering arc from obligation-audience analysis through architecture design, implementation, triad verification, institutionalization, and continuous iteration.
2. Demonstrate inclusion-mastery judgment applying accessibility-as-architecture, evidence-over-checklist task-parity validation, and curb-cut universal-benefit recognition across the consolidated foundations-WCAG-AT-markup-alternatives-color-motor-cognitive-patterns-systems-interaction-testing-ecosystem-governance knowledge.
3. Integrate Gestalt-load-graphics-D3-spatial-IA-latency-math-EDA-embedding-narrative-semiographic-executive disciplines into universally accessible visualization systems.
4. Prevent canonical failures (overlay delusion, alt theater, color-only encoding, keyboard traps, checklist ceilings, silent updates, announcement floods, equivalence burial, simulation theater, governance vacuum, fleet drift, AI laundering) through documented prevention systems.
5. And uphold access-as-right, honest-limitation, nothing-about-us-without-us, privacy-dignity, and universal-design ethics climbing toward accessibility leadership with governance institutionalization and standards participation.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the consolidated capability map — the accessibility foundations (the social-model framing, standards-law landscape, AT partnership map, maturity model), the WCAG depth (the POUR criteria applied to charts with conformance mechanics and gap navigation), the AT mechanics (the accessibility-tree-to-announcement pipelines, screen-reader-magnification-motor-cognitive experience models, semantics engineering), the markup systems (the accessible SVG-canvas-ARIA architecture, reading-order engineering, generated-semantics automation, multimodal equivalence), the alternative representations (the tiered descriptions, structured tables, sonification, tactile and AI-generation with verification), the color-perceptual systems (the CVD-safe palettes, contrast engineering, redundant encoding, temporal access gating), the motor-operation systems (the keyboard-complete interaction, focus management, target-timing-tolerance engineering), the cognitive systems (the plain language, load management, predictability-tolerance, neurodiversity-informed design), the chart-pattern library (the five-dimension per-type patterns across families), the system-level architecture (the dashboard-table-report-live-mobile-workflow inclusion), the interaction systems (the live regions, dynamic content, equivalence translation, custom widgets), the testing evidence systems (the automated-expert-experiential triad, conformance reporting, program strategy), the ecosystem fluency (the library-framework-tooling-supply-chain navigation), and the governance institutions (the policy-role-process-measurement-culture systems) as one inclusive-visualization discipline; the capstone workflow — the master arc: context-and-obligation-analysis (the audience-disability-profile-and-legal-obligation assessment: the public-sector-commercial-and-internal-tool requirement differences, the WCAG-level-and-scope definition with the maturity-stage positioning), architecture-and-pattern-design (the representation-suite design: the semantics-description-table-sonification composition by chart classes from the pattern library, the interaction-equivalence architecture: the keyboard-focus-live-region systems for the product's interaction inventory, the color-typography-motion inclusion specifications: the verified-palette-contrast-and-gating standards), implementation (the semantic markup and component engineering: the SVG-canvas-ARIA systems with framework integration, the alternative-representation delivery: the description-table-and-control implementations with generation automation where scale demands, the interaction-translation build: the keyboard equivalents and announcement choreography for every pointer interaction), verification (the testing-triad execution: the automated CI scanning, expert AT audits against the visualization-specific protocols, disabled-user task testing with representative participants, the equivalence-matrix completion: the every-capability-has-an-accessible-path verification from the system discipline), institutionalization (the conformance documentation: the accessibility statement, audit reports, and remediation roadmap, the governance integration: the acceptance criteria, review checklists, monitoring, and issue-SLA establishment, the knowledge capture: the pattern-library and decision-record contributions) and iteration (the field telemetry and feedback loops: the barrier-report and accommodation-request channels operationalized, the continuous conformance maintenance: the regression monitoring and re-audit cadences) as the complete inclusion-engineering cycle; the integration thesis — the inclusion-mastery judgment: accessibility-as-architecture-not-accommodation (the semantics-equivalence-and-operation systems designed-in from wireframes versus retrofitted after audits — the maturity-model culmination, the accessible-by-default component and generation infrastructure making inclusion the path of least resistance), the evidence-over-checklist standard (the criterion compliance necessary but task-parity sufficient — the can-users-actually-extract-insights-and-complete-journeys validation as the true acceptance test, the experiential-truth primacy: the disabled-user findings overriding tool-green complacency), and the universal-benefit recognition (the curb-cut dividend: the direct labels, data tables, keyboard paths, plain language, and reduced motion improving every user's experience — the inclusion-as-quality identity from the culture layer) as the mature inclusion judgment; cross-course integration — the curriculum position: Gestalt and preattentive science (courses 1-2) providing the grouping-salience principles whose redundant-encoding and annotation companions this discipline mandates, cognitive load (course 3) as the shared foundation — the working-memory and progressive-disclosure systems serving cognitive accessibility directly, graphics APIs (course 4) as the canvas-WebGL medium whose opacity this discipline remedies through parallel structures and full equivalence, D3-SVG craft (course 5) whose join-driven ARIA, focus patterns, and reduced-motion gating were built as accessibility-first systems, spatial-3D (course 6) consuming the map-globe-3D equivalence mandates and region-list alternatives, information architecture (course 7) as the structural partner — the landmark-heading-wayfinding systems shared between navigation design and AT traversal, latency engineering (course 8) intersecting through timing-adjustable controls, announcement throttling, and performance-equity commitments, linear algebra (course 10) as neutral computation whose outputs inherit the encoding-accessibility decisions, EDA (course 11) workflows requiring accessible exploration for disabled analysts, dimensionality reduction (course 12) embedding displays needing summary-first and table-companion treatment for high-dimensional complexity, scrollytelling (course 13) consuming the step-navigation-announcement and reduced-motion narrative-parity systems, semiographics (course 14) whose symbol-metaphor designs carry the plain-language and cultural-clarity obligations, and executive communication (course 15) whose high-stakes audiences include disabled executives and whose reports inherit the accessible-document generation requirements; case synthesis — the worked systems (the public-health-dashboard accessibility program — the fleet-scale governance with generated descriptions, table companions, and WCAG-AA conformance across hundreds of charts, the financial-analytics-platform inclusion engineering — the complex-widget keyboard systems, live-region choreography, and disabled-user-tested exploration journeys, the newsroom-data-journalism accessible graphics — the scrollytelling step-parity, alt-text craft, and representation-suite provisions under deadline workflows, the enterprise-BI-suite accessibility transformation — the legacy remediation through component-library replacement with governance institutionalization) decomposed through the capstone workflow; failure post-mortems — the canonical errors (the overlay-widget delusion, alt-text theater, color-only encoding, keyboard-trap charts, compliance-checklist ceiling, silent updates, announcement floods, equivalence burial, simulation theater, governance vacuum, fleet drift, AI-description laundering) with the prevention systems from sections 1-14; the maturity model — stage one (the standards-and-AT-literate practitioner: the WCAG fluency with screen-reader operational competence), stage two (the inclusive-implementation engineer: the semantics-equivalence-keyboard-cognitive systems built into charts and dashboards), stage three (the inclusion-architect: the system-level equivalence design with testing-triad mastery and pattern-library stewardship), stage four (the accessibility-leader: the governance institutionalization, culture shaping, disabled-community partnership, and standards participation) as the capability roadmap; the inclusion-ethics position — the professional standard: the access-as-right commitment (the accessibility-as-human-right-not-feature framing from the legal-and-ethical foundations: the exclusion-as-harm recognition), the honest-limitation communication (the known-gap transparency in statements and documentation: the workaround-and-timeline provisions without excuse-making, the alternative-recommendation duty when chart types resist access), the nothing-about-us-without-us practice (the disabled-user participation as research-and-governance obligation not consultation theater: the compensated-partnership standards), the privacy-and-dignity respect (the AT-detection-and-accommodation-data ethics: the preference-based-adaptation over surveillance-style detection, the disability-status-privacy in testing and telemetry), and the universal-design aspiration (the single-experience-serving-the-widest-range ideal: the separate-but-equal-alternatives as bridge not destination) as the inclusion ethics; and the capstone — a complete universally-accessible visualization system: select a real data-product context with genuine accessibility obligations, run the obligation-and-audience analysis defining conformance targets and maturity positioning, design the complete inclusion architecture (representation suites, interaction equivalence, perceptual standards per the pattern and system libraries), implement the semantic-alternative-interaction systems within the chosen framework and library ecosystem, verify through the automated-expert-experiential triad with equivalence-matrix completion and disabled-user task validation, institutionalize through statements-governance-monitoring-knowledge-capture, and deliver the system with the conformance evidence package, remediation roadmap for known gaps, and ethics-compliant participation documentation.
