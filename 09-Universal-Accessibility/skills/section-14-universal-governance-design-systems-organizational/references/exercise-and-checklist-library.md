# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Scale accessible visualization organizationally by establishing policy and strategy (written commitments with enforceable tiers
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Accessibility scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** scale accessible visualization organizationally by establishing policy and strategy (written commitments with enforceable tiers, assessment-to-roadmap cycles, legal-procurement alignment, resource models), building the people architecture (center-of-excellence, champion, and hybrid models, role-specific responsibilities across design-development-product-QA-data, tiered competency development, incentive structures with disabled professional inclusion), governing through design systems as the primary lever (verified component libraries with release gates, accessible token distribution, do-don't-verify documentation with rationale, maintenance and migration discipline), integrating process across the lifecycle (shift-left requirements-design-development-test gates, agile definition-of-done inclusion, release checklists with exception governance, incident response, supply chain verification), instrumenting accountability (coverage-quality-experience-leading metric sets, executive-team-external reporting, gaming-resistant targets, maturity benchmarking), cultivating culture (limits-respecting empathy, guilds and rituals, advocacy coalitions, hero-independent sustainability), and preventing policy-without-teeth, ivory-tower, bypass, checkbox, hero-dependence, debt-spiral, vendor-blindness, and metric-theater failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.
