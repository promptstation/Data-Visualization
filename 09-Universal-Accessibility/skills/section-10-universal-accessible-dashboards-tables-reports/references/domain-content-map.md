# Domain Content Map — Accessible Dashboards, Tables, and Reports: System-Level Inclusion

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Architect accessible dashboard systems through AT-navigable IA (landmark-heading structures, priority-ordered DOM, panel indexes), accessible global controls with live-region effect announcements and exploration safety, panel-level pattern composition with freshness context, and session-state persistence.
2. Master data-table craft (semantic completeness, large-table navigation with sticky-context and responsive transformations, virtualized-table semantics under recycling, chart-table synchronization orchestration, export integrity).
3. Engineer accessible reports and documents (heading-reading-order structure, tagged-PDF pipelines with chart alternatives, HTML-first format strategy, template-baked automation, presentation accommodations).
4. Build live-system access (throttled-significant-change announcements, multimodal severity-clear alerts with history review, wall-display companion equivalence, operational-journey verification).
5. Maintain mobile accessibility (breakpoint preservation, touch-target-gesture equivalence, VoiceOver-TalkBack integration, cross-device continuity).
6. Include complete analytics workflows (end-to-end journeys, self-service builder-query equivalents, collaboration-sharing, admin-meta-accessibility, data-preparation).
7. Govern system-level equivalence through coverage matrices, cross-component consistency, progressive-enhancement fallbacks, accessibility observability, and continuous maintenance gates, preventing component-only-burial-lighthouse-responsive-regression-workflow-cliff-admin-ghetto-meta-fleet-drift anti-patterns.
