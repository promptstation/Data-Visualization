## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Defend against embedding misreadings through spatial fallacy literacy (between-cluster distance, size-density, global structure, axis-coordinate, and scale-zoom errors with original-space verification remediation).
2. Detect invented structure (noise crystallization with negative controls, preprocessing-batch-imputation ghosts, sampling-selection artifacts, confirmation bias amplification with parameter fishing detection, authenticity verification protocols), prevent relational misreadings (unaligned cross-method and cross-dataset comparison errors, temporal drift versus recomputation confusion, quantitative overreach in coordinate arithmetic and precision illusions, communication failures from caveat omission through authority transfer, verification checklists), master domain-specific fallacies (single-cell purity-trajectory-batch-resolution errors, text semantic-anisotropy-reification hazards, business segment-outlier-KPI overreach, scientific exploratory-reproducibility-trend failures, domain literacy obligations).
3. Operate the consolidated defense system (pre-publication checklists, team institutionalization with review protocols and artifact galleries, personal first-question-multi-view-documentation-humility habits, defense evolution with postmortems and community contribution, taxonomy mastery assessment), and avoid taxonomy ignorance, checklist ritualism, caveat burial, fallacy projection, defensive paralysis, and literacy stagnation meta-failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the consolidated misinterpretation defense system — the failure modes that make embeddings lie and the literacy that prevents it: the spatial fallacy family — distance and geometry misreadings: the between-cluster distance fallacy (the error of reading t-SNE and UMAP gaps as similarity statements when layout arrangement does not preserve input relationships, from the interpretation contracts of earlier sections; the cluster proximity inference hazard where "these groups are related" claims rest on nothing but layout position; the remediation discipline requiring original-space distance verification before any between-group similarity claim); the cluster size and density fallacies (the area-equals-size misreading where embedding cluster extents are assumed proportional to population counts, contradicted by the critique literature; the density-equals-concentration misreading where point density in layouts is assumed to reflect input density, the finding that motivated density-preserving extensions; the within-cluster structure overreading that treats sub-patterns inside clusters as meaningful geometry without stability verification); the global structure fallacies (the macro arrangement interpretation error reading overall layout shape as data structure when global preservation is partial at best; the gradient direction assumption reading left-to-right or center-to-edge progress without original-data trajectory verification; the initialization global illusion where arrangement inherited from PCA initialization is mistaken for method-preserved structure); the axis and coordinate fallacies (the axis meaning invention assigning interpretations to embedding dimensions that have no intrinsic semantics, unlike PCA's loadings-based axes; the coordinate value comparison error treating layout coordinates as measurable quantities for arithmetic or ranking; the orientation significance assumption ignoring rotation and reflection freedom); and the scale and zoom fallacies (the zoom level structure invention where apparent clusters form at specific magnifications from point density artifacts, the aspect ratio distortion blindness where stretched displays alter perceived structure, the resolution dependence ignorance where different structures appear at different rendering densities) as the spatial family; the invented pattern family — structure authenticity misreadings: the method-invented structure (the uniform noise producing clusters phenomenon documented in the artifact research literature where stochastic neighbor methods crystallize random data; the negative control discipline requiring random and shuffled data embeddings as baseline verification; the parameter-manufactured patterns where perplexity and neighbor settings split and merge groups by configuration rather than data); the preprocessing artifact structure (the batch and technical effect separation where non-substantive variance drives layout; the scaling and transformation ghosts where normalization choices create or destroy apparent groups; the imputation and missing pattern leakage where data handling decisions become visible as structure; the duplicate and artifact clumping where data quality failures form dense spots); the sampling and selection artifacts (the sample composition structure where subset selection biases appear as data groups; the survivorship and coverage holes where missing populations distort layout interpretation; the temporal window artifacts where time-bounded samples create boundary structure); the confirmation bias amplification (the pattern seeking perception where humans see hoped-for structure in ambiguous layouts; the parameter fishing documentation gap where sweeping until the preferred picture appears hides its own search; the narrative imposition fitting stories to layout shapes without original-data evidence); and the authenticity verification protocol (the original space confirmation requirement verifying every claimed structure in input features, the cross-method and stability triangulation from the evaluation systems, the synthetic and negative control calibration establishing what each method invents, the expert adversarial review deliberately hunting artifacts in consequential claims) as the authenticity layer; the relational fallacy family — comparison and communication misreadings: the cross-embedding comparison errors (the unaligned layout comparison fallacy juxtaposing embeddings with different rotations, scales, and parameters as if directly comparable; the cross-method geometry comparison error reading t-SNE versus UMAP visual differences as data differences rather than objective differences; the cross-dataset comparison hazard comparing embedding shapes from different data and preprocessing meaninglessly); the temporal comparison fallacies (the drift versus recomputation artifact confusion where layout changes from refitting are mistaken for data evolution; the window boundary structure drift where streaming embedding edges create false change signals; the version upgrade ghost changes where library and parameter updates alter layouts interpreted as structure shift); the quantitative claim overreach (the embedding coordinate arithmetic computing distances, ratios, and statistics on layout coordinates as if metric valid; the cluster count precision illusion claiming exact group numbers from visually counted blobs without stability evidence; the percentage and proportion invention quantifying group sizes from embedding areas); the communication failure modes (the caveat omission leaving preservation limits undisclosed in captions and presentations; the method and parameter opacity preventing evaluation and replication; the visual seduction exploitation where beautiful embeddings persuade beyond evidence strength; the authority transfer assuming algorithm complexity guarantees result validity); and the communication verification checklist (the claim-evidence alignment review tracing every statement to validated structure, the caveat completeness check confirming distance, density, global, and axis limitations are disclosed, the reproducibility package availability standard, the audience comprehension testing confirming readers can avoid the fallacies given the annotations) as the relational layer; the domain-specific fallacy collection — the applied misreading patterns: the single-cell context fallacies (the cell type purity assumption reading embedding clusters as clean populations while ignoring doublets, ambient signal, and continuum cells; the trajectory overconfidence reading pseudotime paths as differentiation truth without marker corroboration; the batch-as-biology error interpreting technical separation as cell type difference; the resolution cherry-picking tuning parameters until expected cell types appear); the text and embedding context fallacies (the semantic space overliteralism reading embedding geometry as precise meaning relationships; the anisotropy blindness where cone effects in text embeddings shape layouts unrecognized; the topic cluster reification reading document groupings as discrete topics while ignoring overlap and continuum); the business analytics fallacies (the segment reification marketing embedding clusters as customer types without original feature profiling validation; the outlier villain narratives storied as fraud or error without investigation; the KPI embedding authority lending false rigor to weak underlying data); the scientific research fallacies (the exploratory-as-confirmatory presentation reporting embedding-generated hypotheses as tested findings; the irreproducible figure chains publishing embeddings without seeds, versions, and preprocessing; the method trend following applying field defaults without fitness assessment); and the domain literacy obligation (the field critique literature consumption maintaining domain-specific artifact knowledge, the domain expert consultation triggers recognizing when interpretation exceeds analyst competence, the cross-domain fallacy transfer awareness recognizing analogous errors across fields) as the domain layer; the consolidated pitfall defense system — the protection architecture: the pre-publication defense checklist (confirmation that original space verification is complete, that stability and parameter sensitivity evidence is attached, that negative controls and synthetic calibration have run, that caveats, parameters, and reproducibility packages are complete, and that cross-method or expert review triangulation has occurred); the team defense institutionalization (the embedding review protocols placing peer verification gates on consequential displays, the fallacy literacy training embedding misreading taxonomies in onboarding, the artifact gallery maintenance as living reference collections of known failures, the checklist integration in pipelines through automated reminders and gates); the personal defense habits (the first question reflex asking what could make this structure fake before any finding claim, the multi-view habit automatically comparing PCA, t-SNE, UMAP, and sweeps, the documentation discipline recording evidence concurrently, the humility calibration matching confidence language to validation strength); the defense evolution practices (the new artifact knowledge integration flowing critique literature findings into checklists, the postmortem learning analyzing misreading incidents to strengthen defenses, the community contribution sharing local fallacy discoveries at awareness); and the taxonomy mastery assessment (the fallacy recognition testing through identify-the-misreading exercises on example figures, the defense completeness audits periodically reviewing checklist coverage against the consolidated taxonomy) as the defense layer; and the section meta-antipatterns — the failure of failures library: the taxonomy ignorance claiming artifact literacy without knowing the documented fallacies, remedied by the mastery assessment discipline; the checklist ritualism completing defense boxes without genuine verification, remedied by the adversarial review and postmortem culture; the caveat burial hiding limitations in supplementary materials while headlines overclaim, remedied by the prominence and comprehension standards; the fallacy projection accusing methods of artifacts that are actually preprocessing or data failures, remedied by the diagnosis protocol sequencing; the defensive paralysis refusing all embedding use for fear of misreading, remedied by the validated use confidence from the evaluation systems; and the literacy stagnation relying on years-old artifact knowledge as methods and critiques evolve, remedied by the defense evolution practices, with detection methods as the meta-discipline.
