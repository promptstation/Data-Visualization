## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Engineer embeddings at scale through challenge mapping (complexity profiles with approximation thresholds, visualization-side limits, quality-at-scale questions, strategy families), deploy acceleration toolkits (Barnes-Hut-FIt-SNE-negative-sampling methods, ANN-GPU neighbor search with recall management, cuML implementations with fidelity verification, PCA-random prereduction leverage, approximate-versus-exact verification discipline), solve out-of-sample extension (UMAP-transform and Nyström-landmark approaches, nearest-neighbor assignment, parametric alternatives, frozen-layout conventions with version compatibility, extension verification and shift monitoring).
2. Build incremental-streaming systems (windowed-reservoir-mini-batch approaches, cross-time Procrustes alignment and drift detection, pipeline orchestration with embedding stores, streaming honesty disclosures), compose multiresolution strategies (subsample-embed-project patterns, coarse-to-fine pyramids with zoom switching, aggregated-representative displays, distributed partitioning with boundary awareness), practice scale engineering (capacity planning with feasibility matrices, benchmark-load testing with degradation thresholds, operational runbooks, engineering anti-pattern vigilance), and avoid complexity oblivion, unverified approximation, extension neglect, drift chaos, window blindness, resolution confusion, capacity surprise, and chain opacity failures.
