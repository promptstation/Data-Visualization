## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Synthesize complete embedding practice by executing the full lifecycle across all fourteen preceding sections with phase-mastery checkpoints, cross-section integration fluency from mechanics through governance, and uncertainty-calibrated practitioner judgment.
2. Diagnose embedding health through layout-symptom-to-cause mapping with systematic isolation and reference comparison, validation-gap audits against evidence pyramids, pipeline reproducibility-performance-version triage, communication-failure analysis, and meta-cognitive retrospective habits.
3. Demonstrate command through capstone vehicles (complete novel-dataset investigations, method comparison studies, production-scale pipelines.
4. Audit-remediation exercises, communication-education artifacts) evaluated on lifecycle-validation-artifact-literacy-reproducibility-communication-anti-pattern rubrics.
5. And extend forward through methods frontiers, domain specializations, engineering-platform directions, and scholarship-community paths under the embedding-skeptic-with-confidence craft identity, consolidating every section's anti-pattern library into the final prevention system.
