## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Ground dimensionality-reduction practice in the problem foundations (display limits, distance-concentration-volume-hubness curses, intrinsic-dimension manifold hypothesis with testing awareness, structure-preservation framing with lossy-compression honesty), navigate the method taxonomy (selection-extraction, linear-nonlinear, global-local preservation goals with method mapping, parametric-nonparametric and deterministic-stochastic spectrums), place practice in historical lineage (PCA-MDS statistical heritage, manifold-learning revolution, SNE-t-SNE stochastic family, UMAP-scaling-critique modern era, deep-embedding frontiers).
2. Map application landscapes (single-cell, text, image, general multivariate, communication contexts).
3. Adopt the practitioner stance (question-before-method routing, preprocessing-foundation obligations, skepticism-verification-artifact-literacy discipline, honest-communication commitments), and avoid embedding-first reflexes, curse ignorance, manifold blindness, taxonomy confusion, history amnesia, application copying, and trust defaults.
