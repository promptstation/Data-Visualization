# Domain Content Map — t-SNE Mechanics: From Stochastic Neighbor Embedding to the Crowded Solution

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Master t-SNE mechanics through the SNE foundation (Gaussian conditional affinities with perplexity-matched bandwidths, symmetrized joint probabilities, low-dimensional q-analogs, KL-divergence objective with asymmetry consequences, early-exaggeration phases), understand the crowding problem and t-distribution solution (capacity-mismatch argument, heavy-tail leniency geometry, cluster-formation-density-distortion properties, objective alternatives).
2. Operate optimization mechanics (attraction-repulsion gradients, initialization strategies with PCA-init research findings, iteration-learning-rate convergence management, Barnes-Hut quadtree approximation with theta tradeoffs, FIt-SNE-GPU scaling), command perplexity (effective-neighbor semantics with adaptive bandwidths, effect atlas from fragmentation through merging, interaction effects with size and initialization, sweep diagnostics with persistence discipline, multiscale variants).
3. Read outputs under the interpretation contract (neighborhood-cluster-local-density preservations, distance-size-density-global distortions, verification workflows with quantitative backup, communication caveats and parameter disclosure), and avoid single-perplexity publishing, cluster-distance measuring.
4. Run-variance ignorance, exaggeration misreading, learning-rate autopilot, big-data exactness, objective blindness, and sweep fishing failures.
