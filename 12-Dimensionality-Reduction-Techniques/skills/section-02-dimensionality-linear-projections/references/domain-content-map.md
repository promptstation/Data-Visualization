Integrate into modern pipelines (pca-init patterns, pre-reduction-for-scale standards, hybrid displays, parametric out-of-sample advantages, variance-annotation obligations), and avoid nonlinear jumps, scaling neglect, arbitrary components, stress ignorance, Shepard omission, rotation errors, init blindness, and variance-importance fallacies.
