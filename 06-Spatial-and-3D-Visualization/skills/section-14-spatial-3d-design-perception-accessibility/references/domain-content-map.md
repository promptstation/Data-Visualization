# Domain Content Map — Design, Perception, and Accessibility for Spatial Visualization

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Synthesize cartographic design (visual hierarchy with figure-ground data dominance, geographic color systems, label typography conventions, furniture composition) with spatial perception science (mental-model formation, depth and scale perception, wayfinding cognition, dual-task load management), visualize uncertainty through boundary fuzz, confidence displays, precision-appropriate symbols, and staleness indication under layered-disclosure balance.
2. Build spatial accessibility (keyboard and screen-reader map navigation, data-equivalent provisions, 3D alternatives and motion gating, CVD-contrast palettes, assistive-technology testing).
3. Adapt design responsively and contextually (mobile patterns, embed and print modes, audience calibration, cultural and local sensitivity), craft narrative annotation and map-sequence storytelling with guided-exploration balance.
4. Operate design-review systems (audit checklists, task-based critique, failure catalogs) within ethics frameworks (map-power awareness, location privacy, representation equity), and institutionalize through style systems, component libraries, and cross-discipline collaboration.
