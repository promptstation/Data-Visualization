# Domain Content Map — Terrain, Globes, Buildings, and Digital Twins: Geo-3D Rendering

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Render terrain through DEM pipelines (quantized-mesh-terrain-RGB tiles, heightmap displacement, hillshade-hypsometric craft) with vertical-exaggeration disclosure.
2. Build urban 3D from footprint extrusion and instanced million-building rendering with LoD hierarchies and tileset streaming.
3. Construct globes (Three.js sphere-texture approaches, great-circle arcs, Cesium-MapLibre native modes) under globe-versus-flat task decisions, architect digital twins with telemetry-to-visual synchronization and fidelity honesty, render point clouds via Potree-3D-Tiles octree streaming.
4. Integrate indoor-BIM multi-floor views.
5. Execute geo-to-3D coordinate registration (ENU-ECEF frames, precision management, layer alignment), deploy scientific volumetric and subsurface rendering with uncertainty representation, extend to WebXR immersive contexts under comfort discipline, and engineer production through asset compression, progressive loading, device-tier degradation, and performance telemetry.
