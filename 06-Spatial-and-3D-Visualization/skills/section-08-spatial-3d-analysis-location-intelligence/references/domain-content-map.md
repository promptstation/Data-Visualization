# Domain Content Map — Spatial Analysis and Location Intelligence: Buffers, Isochrones, Routing, and Spatial Joins

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module

authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Execute spatial analysis through the OGC predicate-operation vocabulary using turf-js client-side toolkits (geodesic measurements, buffers, boolean operations, point-in-polygon aggregation) within client-compute limits, engineer spatial joins including areal interpolation for mismatched geographies with quality validation.
2. Build proximity analyses (multi-ring buffers, dissolve coverage) and isochrone accessibility systems (network-aware reachability, opportunity counting, nested-contour rendering with uncertainty acknowledgment).
3. Integrate routing engines with route-rendering and network-distance literacy.
4. Operate geocoding pipelines with confidence scoring and caching, compute regional statistics with normalization, small-count suppression, hotspot awareness, and uncertainty propagation, escalate to PostGIS-spatial-SQL and geopandas server tiers in hybrid precompute-refine architectures.
5. Apply site-selection-coverage-equity workflow patterns, and enforce analysis integrity through MAUP sensitivity checks, boundary-effect awareness, reproducibility documentation, and ecological-inference caution.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the analysis-computation layer: the spatial-analysis position — the compute-on-geography discipline: the visualization-analysis integration thesis (the interactive maps inviting analytic questions — the what's-nearby, what's-within-reach, how-does-it-flow queries that demand computation not just rendering, the analysis-in-the-browser versus server-side division of labor), the OGC-simple-features operation vocabulary (the contains-intersects-within-overlaps-touches-crosses spatial predicates, the buffer-intersection-union-difference geometric operations) as the operation foundation; the turf-js client-side toolkit — the browser GIS: the measurement operations (the distance-area-length-bearing computations with the geodesic-correctness from the coordinates discipline, the point-line-polygon spatial relations), the transformation operations (the buffer generation — the radius-based influence zones, the clip-mask-intersect-union-difference boolean operations, the simplify-transform pipelines), the aggregation operations (the points-within-polygon counting, the collect-and-summarize spatial joins, the convex-hull and center-point computations), and the client-compute limits (the dataset-size ceilings for browser analysis — the worker-offload and server-escalation patterns) as the in-browser analysis engine; the spatial-join systems — the attribute-geography merge: the point-in-polygon join mechanics (the observation-to-region assignment — the census-tract-and-administrative-unit enrichment patterns, the boundary-and-multi-region edge cases), the polygon-polygon overlay operations (the intersect-and-identity overlays for region-crossing analysis, the area-weighted interpolation for mismatched-geography joins — the areal-interpolation technique), the performance-optimized joins (the spatial-index acceleration for large-to-large joins, the batch-and-chunk processing patterns), and the join-quality validation (the unmatched-record accounting, the coverage-and-overlap verification before visualization commitment) as the join engineering; the buffer-and-influence analysis — the proximity operations: the buffer construction discipline (the geodesic-buffer correctness at scale, the multi-ring-buffer patterns for distance-band analysis, the buffer-of-line-and-polygon semantics), the service-area approximations (the radius-based catchment analysis and its road-network-blindness limitation motivating the isochrone upgrade), the dissolve-and-union patterns (the overlapping-buffer merging for coverage analysis), and the proximity-storytelling applications (the facilities-within-distance-of-population analyses, the exposure-and-access mapping) as the proximity toolkit; the isochrone-and-accessibility analysis — the reachability computation: the isochrone concept (the equal-travel-time regions replacing naive radius circles — the network-and-traffic-aware reachability, the walk-bike-drive-transit mode distinction), the isochrone-service integration (the API-based generation — the Mapbox-Valhalla-OpenRouteService providers, the client-side approximation options, the multi-time-band nesting — the 5-10-15-minute contour families), the accessibility-metric construction (the opportunity-counting within isochrones — the jobs-healthcare-greenspace access measures, the population-weighted accessibility aggregations), and the isochrone-visualization craft (the nested-contour rendering conventions, the mode-and-time comparisons, the uncertainty-and-traffic-variability acknowledgment) as the reachability system; the routing-and-network analysis — the path computation: the routing-engine integration (the OSRM-Valhalla-Graphhopper-directions-API patterns — the route-geometry-and-duration retrieval, the multi-waypoint and optimization queries at awareness), the route-rendering craft (the path-alternative display, the step-by-step-segment styling, the route-comparison visualizations), the network-analysis concepts (the graph-model-of-streets literacy — the connectivity-and-centrality measures at awareness, the network-versus-euclidean-distance distinctions), and the routing-visualization applications (the logistics-and-delivery displays, the commute-and-migration flows, the emergency-response planning views) as the network layer; the geocoding-and-search-analysis — the location-resolution layer: the batch-geocoding pipelines (the address-list resolution with the rate-limit management, the confidence-scoring and manual-review queues for low-confidence matches, the caching-and-deduplication economics), the reverse-geocoding enrichment (the coordinate-to-context attachment — the neighborhood-and-administrative labeling), the search-UX integration (the typeahead-and-autocomplete patterns, the result-biasing by map-viewport and user-context, the no-result-and-ambiguous-result handling) as the resolution craft; the aggregation-and-statistics layer — the spatial summarization: the regional-statistics computation (the group-by-geography summarizations with the normalization discipline from the thematic section, the small-count-suppression privacy floors), the spatial-statistics awareness (the clustering-detection — the Moran's-I and hotspot concepts at awareness, the distance-decay patterns), the time-varying aggregations (the temporal-binning of events by geography — the animation-and-small-multiple time-series maps), and the uncertainty-propagation practice (the confidence-interval and margin-of-error display conventions for estimated spatial statistics — the error-visualization patterns) as the summarization discipline; the server-side-and-database analysis — the scale tier: the PostGIS literacy (the spatial-SQL operations — the ST_-function family for joins, buffers, and aggregations at database scale, the spatial-index configuration), the analysis-API patterns (the REST-and-GraphQL spatial endpoints, the precomputed-analysis caching strategies), the Python-R analysis pipelines (the geopandas-sf ecosystems for heavy spatial analysis feeding web visualization — the analysis-render division of labor), and the hybrid architectures (the server-precompute with client-refine patterns, the analysis-on-demand endpoints) as the scale escalation; the location-intelligence application patterns — the applied synthesis: the site-selection workflow (the catchment-demographics-competition-traffic composite analysis maps), the service-coverage optimization (the facility-location-and-gap-analysis visualizations), the incident-and-event analysis (the spatiotemporal-cluster detection displays), the mobility-and-access equity analysis (the population-weighted-accessibility comparisons across neighborhoods) as the professional application library; the analysis-integrity discipline — the rigor layer: the MAUP-and-scale sensitivity checks (the multi-resolution analysis comparison from the thematic discipline), the edge-and-boundary effects (the coastal-and-border buffer truncations, the zone-assignment ambiguities), the reproducibility practice (the parameter-and-version documentation for analysis pipelines, the deterministic-seed discipline for randomized operations), and the ecological-inference caution (the area-level-pattern versus individual-behavior interpretation boundaries) as the analytic-honesty standard; and the spatial-analysis deliverable — the buffer-join-isochrone-route-aggregate computation capability integrated with visualization.
