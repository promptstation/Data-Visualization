# Domain Content Map — Synthesis and Capstone: The Complete Story Craft

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Synthesize complete story craft by executing the full scrollytelling lifecycle across all fourteen preceding sections with parallel iteration reality, state-machine and beat unifying architectures, and developed craft judgment through pattern internalization and tradeoff intuition.
2. Diagnose story health through experience symptom analysis tracing abandonment-confusion-flatness to causes, technical triage across jank-mobile-accessibility-state failures, narrative health audits of evidence-ethics-honesty alignment, production process diagnosis, and meta-cognitive retrospective practices.
3. Demonstrate command through capstone vehicles (complete original data stories with editorial packages, format mastery comparisons, technical deep dives with budget evidence, story audit remediation, editorial production simulations) evaluated on narrative-choreography-technical-inclusion-ethics-audience-anti-pattern rubrics.
4. And extend forward through format evolution, craft deepening paths, leadership-community directions, and portfolio-identity development under the storyteller-with-engineering-rigor and reader-advocate craft identity, consolidating every section's anti-pattern library into the final prevention system.
