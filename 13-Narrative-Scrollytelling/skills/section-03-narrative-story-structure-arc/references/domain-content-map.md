# Domain Content Map — Story Structure and Arc: Dramaturgy for Data

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Architect data story structure through arc foundations (three-act adaptation with evidence-accumulation substitution, Hullman-martini-glass and Segel-Heer research structures, mystery-journey-argument-tour-day-in-life data arc patterns, beat-structure craft with one-revelation granularity, arc selection by story type-audience-data shape).
2. Design openings (curiosity-human-stakes-visual hook mechanics, orientation obligations establishing contract and scope, anti-pattern awareness, first-fifteen-seconds testing).
3. Build middles (progressive disclosure laddering with just-in-time context, tension-release rhythm with complications and midpoint turns, evidence accumulation through induction-deduction-triangulation with counterexample honesty, transition craft, sag diagnosis through analytics and repetition audits), craft endings (climax positioning aligned with peak visuals, synthesis-so-what-human-return-action resolution components, martini-glass exploration release, ending anti-pattern avoidance, full-arc review protocols), and avoid chronology defaults, hook absence, middle sag, complexity cliffs, payoff burial, resolution omission, and arc rigidity failures.
