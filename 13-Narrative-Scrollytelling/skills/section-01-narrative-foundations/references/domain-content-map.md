# Domain Content Map — Narrative Foundations: Why Data Stories and the Storytelling Tradition

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Ground scrollytelling practice in narrative foundations through the advantage evidence (transportation-immersion research, memory and emotional engagement mechanisms, causal structure benefits, data-specific narrative case with exploration fatigue reality, Segel-Heer taxonomy and Hullman research base, story-fit judgment criteria), inherit the data journalism lineage (NYT-Snow Fall-ProPublica-Guardian heritage, Pudding-Bloomberg-FT digital evolution, show-don't-tell-evidence-first-reader-respect principles, beyond-journalism applications).
2. Construct story elements (character-subject decisions with data-as-character devices, stakes-tension construction with mystery and change structures, evidence-as-plot-points discipline, voice-tone decisions, elements integration with outline-first sequencing).
3. Operate the narrative design process (discovery through exploration with audience definition.
4. Design through arc-beat-labor-division planning, production through prototype-readthrough-iteration cycles), and avoid narrative washing, story-over-evidence, engagement assumption, genre blindness, audience vagueness, and tradition ignorance failures.
