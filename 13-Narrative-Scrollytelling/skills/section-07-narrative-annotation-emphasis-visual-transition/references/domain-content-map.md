# Domain Content Map — Annotation, Emphasis, and Visual Transition Design: Directing the Reader's Eye

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Direct reader attention through annotation systems (type vocabulary from direct labels through icons, layering discipline with density and hierarchy management, narrative timing with persistence and build sequences, style systems with typographic, color, and connector conventions, and honesty obligations covering sources, uncertainty, and interpretation separation), engineer emphasis (highlight-dim patterns with selective color, size-position-z-order techniques with integrity cautions, motion emphasis integration, typographic cross-references, multi-technique composition with intensity calibration and fatigue prevention, and glance-and-readthrough testing).
2. Design visual transitions (type selection matching change character, shared-element continuity with identity tracking and spatial memory, context maintenance through persistent anchors and comparison affordances, chart-type-scale-scope strategies with impossible-transition recognition, and comprehension-timing-scrollback testing).
3. Integrate composition discipline (per-step emphasis plans with budgets, visual hierarchy and reading-path design, cross-step narrative threads with motifs and payoffs, style-guide token systems with accessible defaults, and quality checks for intent, clutter, and consistency), and avoid annotation sprawl, stale drift, emphasis monotony, invisible dimming, impossible morphs, misleading emphasis, accessibility blindness, and untested attention failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the emphasis craft controlling what readers notice, understand, and remember at each story moment: the annotation foundations — the labeling architecture: the vocabulary of annotation types (direct labels placed at data identify series and points without legend lookups, following the labeling traditions; callouts with leader lines offset text from specific data elements for dense contexts; threshold and reference lines mark benchmarks, averages, and targets as context; region and range highlights shade significant periods and zones; value labels and data callouts show precise numbers at key points, following quantitative-honesty traditions; and icons and markers flag events and locations at specific points, from modern data-viz practice); the discipline of annotation layering (annotations appear after their base context is established, following the layer sequencing of section 4; density management balances progressive annotation accumulation against clutter risk, drawing on cognitive-load traditions; a hierarchy of primary, secondary, and tertiary emphasis levels receives differentiated visual treatment; and collision-avoidance systems combine label-placement algorithms with manual adjustment practice from the layout traditions); the timing of annotation in narrative (annotations arrive synchronized with relevant text sentences, following the reading rhythm of section 4; persistence decisions distinguish annotations that stay through remaining steps from those appearing for one moment; build sequences accumulate multiple annotations across consecutive steps to tell layered stories; and exit conventions remove annotations gracefully when context shifts, preventing stale markers); the style systems for annotation (typographic hierarchy sets sizing and weight conventions from design-system traditions; color coding matches annotation colors to data series and emphasis palettes for consistency; and connector and background treatments style leader lines and text backdrops for legibility over data, following contrast traditions); and the honesty obligations of annotation (source and methodology notes integrate into annotation systems; uncertainty annotations mark estimated or provisional data with confidence caveats; and interpretation separates editorial commentary visually from data labels) as the annotation foundation; the emphasis technique systems — the engineering of attention: the highlight and dim patterns (focus-plus-context dimming reduces opacity or saturation on non-focused elements to direct attention, from visual-hierarchy traditions; selective color emphasis uses grayscale context with colored focus, applying the preattentive color-channel traditions; and persistence rules keep emphasized elements emphasized across all relevant steps, following state-continuity principles); emphasis through size and position (scale emphasis enlarges focused elements with a data-integrity caution against misleading size changes; z-order emphasis brings focused elements forward in stacking; and repositioning moves focus elements toward the visual center of gravity with continuity transitions from the morph techniques of section 6); the integration of motion emphasis (entrance animations capture attention for new elements, applying the attention-direction function of section 6; pulse and glow techniques flag attention with restraint and accessibility limits from motion-sensitivity considerations; and guided eye-path design orders sequential emphasis to direct reading flow through complex visuals); typographic and textual emphasis (inline bold and color highlighting in narrative steps connects words to visual elements; number emphasis visually weights key figures in both text and annotation systems; and cross-reference links tie text phrases to graphic elements through matching colors and markers); the composition of multiple techniques (emphasis stacks combine dimming, highlighting, animation, and labeling for major revelation moments; intensity calibration matches emphasis weight to beat importance, moving from subtle single techniques for minor steps to full stacks at the climax, following the pacing craft of section 3; and fatigue prevention applies the principle that when everything is emphasized, nothing is emphasized, requiring restraint and variation); and the testing of emphasis (glance-test protocols verify through brief exposure that the intended focus is actually noticed, from the evaluation traditions of section 14; attention-tracking evidence at awareness includes eye-tracking and mouse-movement validation from perception research; and readthrough comprehension checks ask readers to report what they noticed per step) as the emphasis layer; the design of visual transitions — the craft of state change: the selection of transition types (morph transitions suit continuous change, tweening element shape and position when data transforms gradually, from the morph techniques of section 6; add-and-remove transitions suit compositional change, choreographing layer entrance and exit when structure changes, from the transition vocabulary of section 2; camera-move transitions suit spatial change with zoom, pan, and reframe for geographic and scale stories; swap transitions suit categorical change with discrete replacement when contexts shift completely; and hybrid compositions combine morphing with adding for complex state changes); the design of shared-element continuity (identity tracking maintains the visual identity of the same data elements through state changes, applying the key-function discipline of section 6; hero-element transitions move featured items between layout positions to carry attention; and spatial memory preservation changes element positions minimally and predictably to support reader orientation, following continuity cognitive principles); the maintenance of context through transitions (persistent reference elements such as axes, legends, and titles survive transitions as orientation anchors; transition duration scales with change magnitude, keeping small changes quick and large changes deliberate, from the duration principles of section 6; and before-after comparison affordances such as ghost images and toggles preserve access to previous states when transitions would otherwise obscure them); the strategies for chart-type transitions (same-data view changes transform bars to lines to scatters using mapped element correspondences; data-scope transitions move between filtered and full or aggregate and detail views with enter-exit choreography; scale and unit transitions change axis domains while annotations update to maintain orientation; and impossible-transition recognition identifies view changes lacking meaningful element correspondence, requiring cuts or fades instead); and the testing of transitions (comprehension verification asks readers what changed and why, using readthrough protocols; timing tuning checks observed scroll speed against animation duration compatibility, from the timing debugging of section 6; and scrollback quality verifies that reverse scrolling replays transitions cleanly without artifacts, from the state management of section 5) as the transition layer; the integration systems for annotation and emphasis — the composition discipline: the per-step emphasis plan (each narrative beat specifies what is labeled, highlighted, and transitioned, extending the storyboard documentation of section 4; an emphasis budget per step prioritizes limited attention resources, following cognitive-load traditions; and consistency across steps keeps a stable visual language for recurring emphasis patterns, from design-system principles); the composition of visual hierarchy (focal-point sequencing establishes primary, secondary, and tertiary attention targets within each step state; reading-path design arranges annotation and emphasis to shape the intended eye journey through the visual, from hierarchy traditions; and rest-and-peak rhythm places quiet steps between emphasis peaks to prevent fatigue, following the tension-release pacing of section 3); the narrative threads across steps (recurring annotation motifs track the same elements across the story with consistent markers, supporting the character and subject continuity devices of section 1; accumulated annotation layers build progressive context where earlier annotations persist as reference; and annotation payoff moments converge accumulated labels in climax visual states, from the climax craft of section 3); the integration with style guides (token systems standardize annotation and emphasis treatments for team consistency, from design-system traditions; reusable components parameterize callout, highlight, and label modules, previewing the tooling traditions of section 13; and accessibility compliance bakes contrast and motion-safe defaults into annotation components, previewing section 11); and the quality checks for integration (emphasis-intent verification audits every annotation and highlight against narrative purpose, applying the purpose framework of section 6; clutter audit protocols review step states for annotation overload and force reduction decisions; and consistency review verifies visual-language uniformity across the complete story) as the integration layer; and the section anti-patterns — the failure library: annotation sprawl, covering charts in unprioritized labels until data disappears, remedied by density management and budget discipline; stale annotation drift, where markers remain after context has shifted and contradict the current state, remedied by exit conventions and state synchronization; emphasis monotony, where every step uses identical highlight treatment and destroys hierarchy, remedied by intensity calibration matched to beat importance; dim-to-invisible, over-dimming context until readers lose orientation, remedied by focus-plus-context balance principles; impossible morphs, attempting element-correspondence-free transitions that produce visual confusion, remedied by transition recognition and cut alternatives; misleading emphasis, where size and color emphasis imply quantitative differences that do not exist, remedied by data-integrity cautions and honesty obligations; accessibility-blind annotation, with low-contrast labels and motion-dependent emphasis excluding users, remedied by compliance integration in styles; and untested attention, assuming intended focus is actual focus without verification, remedied by glance and readthrough testing, with detection methods as the diagnostic.
