# Domain Content Map — Interactivity Within Narrative: Authored Guidance and Reader Freedom

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Balance authored narrative and reader freedom through spectrum mastery (authored-exploratory poles with hybrid territory, martini-glass and slideshow-hybrid structures, engagement-comprehension-integrity benefit analysis, audience-purpose freedom matching, and deliberate position documentation).
2. Design exploratory moments (in-step micro-interactions with boundary discipline, exploration release patterns with sandbox embedding and return affordances, branching forms with convergence and scope discipline, personalized data moments with privacy consciousness, and placement at natural joints with cognitive readiness).
3. Build control and navigation systems (pacing autonomy with play-pause-dwell provisions, chapter menus with step indicators, replay, and skip navigation, progress persistence with deep links and history integration, escape-fallback guarantees, and control discoverability).
4. Implement hybrid experiences (narrative-exploration dual state architecture, reusable interactive components, client-side bounded data provisioning, interaction-path and state-corruption testing, and interactivity analytics with freedom ROI assessment), and avoid freedom illusion, narrative hijack, lost readers, undiscoverable controls, state corruption, branch explosion, trapped experiences, and untested freedom failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the freedom-dimension craft balancing author control with reader exploration inside story experiences: the authored-exploratory spectrum — the design space: the spectrum framework (at one pole sit fully authored linear scroll narratives with no reader choice, the classic scrollytelling form; at the other pole sit free dashboards and visual analytics with no guidance, from the interactive traditions; and most effective data stories live in the hybrid middle where authored structures contain exploratory moments, as the narrative-visualization research describes); the Hullman structures revisited (the martini glass structure, operationalized as a narrow authored opening that widens into exploratory freedom and returns to an authored conclusion, from the research canon; slideshow and micro-interaction hybrids where an authored sequence permits manipulation within steps; and drill-down narrative forms with an authored backbone and optional depth exploration at designated points); the benefit analysis of freedom (engagement research shows reader investment and time spent increase when manipulation is permitted; comprehension tradeoffs appear because exploration aids understanding for motivated readers while losing unstructured ones, from the learning-sciences traditions; and author integrity suffers when readers wander off message, a concern from the journalism traditions); the matching of freedom to audience (general audiences receive low-freedom defaults with guided experiences suited to limited data literacy; expert audiences receive higher-freedom affordances and exploration tools; and purpose calibrates freedom, with persuasion contexts favoring authorship while education contexts welcome exploration); and the documentation of spectrum position (deliberate freedom decisions record explicitly where readers control what, as part of story design from the process of section 3; and the consistency principle keeps interaction affordances predictable throughout rather than appearing as random freedom patches) as the spectrum foundation; the design of exploratory moments — the craft of controlled freedom: micro-interactions within steps (hover and tooltip provisions give reader-initiated detail access within authored states, from the interaction traditions; per-step toggles and filters offer bounded manipulation letting readers test claims, such as removing a category to see its effect; highlight and select affordances permit reader-chosen emphasis within designed visual states; and the boundary discipline ensures controls cannot break narrative flow or contradict story claims); the patterns of exploration release (the martini glass widening implemented as post-climax free exploration sections with full data access; sandbox embeddings mount complete interactive visualizations at story end for motivated readers; guided-to-free transitions reveal controls progressively, teaching readers the tools before unleashing them; and return affordances provide clear paths back to story flow from exploration sections, preventing lost readers); the forms of branching narrative (choose-your-path structures with reader decision points selecting narrative branches, at awareness from the interactive-fiction traditions; conditional content revelation where story elements appear based on reader interactions; branch convergence design where multiple paths rejoin common conclusions to maintain coherence; and branch scope discipline that budgets branching complexity against production reality); personalized data moments (reader input integration with enter-your-own-data calculators letting readers locate themselves in stories, from personalization traditions; comparison affordances with reader-selected cases against story data; and privacy-conscious personalization computing client-side with no tracking by default, previewing the ethics of section 14); and the placement of exploratory moments (freedom belongs at natural joints such as chapter boundaries and post-revelation moments rather than mid-argument; cognitive readiness sequences introduce exploration tools after concepts are established, following progressive-disclosure traditions; and time budgets make depth optional for interested readers without penalizing linear readers) as the moment design layer; the control and navigation systems — the layer of reader agency: pacing control affordances (scroll speed autonomy keeps reader-controlled progression as the format default, from the reader-respect principles of section 2; play and auto-advance options provide hands-free consumption for video-like experiences with user-initiation requirements; and pause-and-dwell tolerance lets the story wait for readers without timeout pressure, previewing the accessibility traditions of section 11); navigation provisions (chapter menus and progress jumping allow direct navigation to story sections for skimmers and returners, from wayfinding traditions; step indicators give visible position markers supporting orientation and jumping; replay and restart controls support re-experiencing stories for sharing and review; and skip-to-content options bypass navigation for keyboard and assistive-technology users, from accessibility standards); state management for agency (reader progress persistence resumes stories where readers left off, from continuity traditions; exploration state separates from narrative state so reader modifications never corrupt story position tracking, extending the state architecture of section 5; share and deep-link states produce specific-moment URLs for social sharing and reference, from distribution traditions; and back-button and history integration respects browser navigation expectations in scroll state management, from web conventions); escape and fallback provisions (exit is always available, guaranteeing no trapped scroll experience per the scroll-jacking prevention of section 2; a static content path keeps the complete story readable without any interaction, from progressive-enhancement discipline; and reduced-experience modes simplify versions for low-bandwidth and reduced-motion contexts, from the accessibility and performance traditions); and the discoverability of controls (affordance visibility makes interactive elements look interactive with consistent visual treatment, from interface traditions; first-use guidance subtly onboards unfamiliar interaction patterns without tutorial bloat; and in-context control documentation signs what readers can do at exploration section entries) as the agency layer; the implementation of interactive narrative — the technical integration: the state architecture for hybrid experiences (dual state management separates narrative state from exploration state, extending the architecture of section 5 for reader modifications; control events from buttons, sliders, and inputs coexist with scroll triggers without conflict; and reset-and-restore mechanics return exploration sections to authored states on scroll-away or reader request); the component patterns for story interactivity (reusable interactive moment components parameterize toggle, filter, and calculator modules, previewing the tooling traditions of section 13; sandbox embedding architecture mounts full visualization components at story end with shared data contexts; and input handling discipline covers form validation and error recovery for reader input moments, from interaction traditions); the data architecture for freedom (client-side data provisioning makes datasets available for reader exploration without server dependencies, from performance traditions; bounded exploration scoping curates subsets sufficient for designed exploration without full database exposure; and in-browser computation patterns handle aggregation and filtering client-side for responsive exploration); the testing of hybrid experiences (interaction path testing verifies that all reader choice combinations produce coherent experiences, extending the testing practices of section 5; state corruption testing stress-tests rapid scrolling combined with interaction to reveal conflicts; and freedom usability testing observes whether readers actually use exploratory affordances effectively, from the evaluation systems of section 14); and the analytics for interactivity (interaction event tracking measures control usage and exploration engagement, from the analytics systems of section 14; path analysis examines reader navigation choices through branching and exploration structures; and freedom ROI assessment justifies interaction investment through engagement and comprehension evidence) as the implementation layer; and the section anti-patterns — the failure library: freedom illusion, offering interactive controls that are purely decorative or change no understanding, remedied by bounded meaningful interaction design; narrative hijack, letting exploration sections contradict or undermine the story's evidence-based claims, remedied by boundary discipline and claim protection; lost in exploration, where readers enter sandboxes and never find the conclusion, remedied by return affordances and placement discipline; control undiscoverability, shipping interactivity nobody notices or understands, remedied by affordance visibility and guidance systems; state corruption chaos, where exploration modifications break narrative position tracking, remedied by dual state separation architecture; branch explosion, building combinatorial narrative paths beyond production and testing capacity, remedied by branch scope discipline; trapped reader experience, scroll-jacking without escape violating reader control, remedied by escape provision guarantees; and untested freedom, launching interactive moments without path and stress testing, remedied by hybrid experience testing protocols, with detection methods as the diagnostic.
