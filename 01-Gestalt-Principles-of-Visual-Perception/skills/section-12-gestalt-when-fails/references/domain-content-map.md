# Domain Content Map — When Gestalt Fails: Illusions, Accidental Patterns, and Grouping Hygiene

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module

authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Detect and prevent accidental groupings (alignment, color-echo, proximity-drift, orphan-legend errors) through systematic composition audits.
2. Manage illusion contexts (size-contrast, curvature, simultaneous-contrast distortions) and moiré-aliasing artifacts with prevention toolkits, guard against false-pattern confabulations (apophenia, over-smoothing, bin arbitrariness) through honest-irregularity practices.
3. Diagnose bistable conflicts and dominance errors through cue-reassignment workflows, and enforce the honesty boundary (grouping reflecting data structure) with upstream data-hygiene verification and cumulative failure-library practice.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the failure and misuse surface: the double-edge principle — the framework reframe: the same automatic organization that makes designed grouping work manufactures structure that is not there (the constructive-perception consequence from sections 1-2: the visual system bets on regularity whether or not the data supports it), making grouping-failure management the defensive half of the discipline; accidental grouping — the unintended-binding catalog: the documented failure family (the accidental-alignment error — unrelated elements collinear or equidistant binding falsely; the color-echo accident — shared hues across unrelated panels creating phantom series; the proximity-drift — labels migrating toward wrong marks through responsive reflow; the orphan-legend syndrome — legend items binding to wrong series through position) with the detection practice (the accidental-pattern audit — scanning compositions for unintended regularities the eye will bind); visual illusions in data graphics — the systematic-distortion layer: the classical geometric illusions with data-graphics relevance (the Müller-Lyer and Ponzo size-context distortions in bar and symbol reading, the Ebbinghaus size-contrast effect in proportional symbols, the Hering and Wundt curvature distortions in grid contexts, the simultaneous-contrast color shifts in heatmaps and choropleths) with the magnitude-awareness (illusion effects small relative to encoding errors but systematic — the cumulative-bias concern in precision reading); the moiré and aliasing artifacts — the sampling failures: the interference patterns from overlapping regular structures (the moiré in layered grids and textured fills, the aliasing in dense line families and small multiples — the Nyquist connection to rendering, the course-4 technical preview) producing phantom motion, texture, and grouping, with the prevention toolkit (the angle-and-frequency separation of layered patterns, the anti-aliasing and downsampling disciplines, the density-reduction alternatives); the false-pattern confabulations — the noise-structure errors: the documented human pattern-hunger (the apophenia tendency — seeing faces in noise, trends in randomness, clusters in uniform scatter) with the visualization-specific risks (the over-smoothing suggestion — spline interpolation implying continuity between sparse points, the bin-size arbitrariness creating and destroying histogram modes, the aspect-ratio slope-manipulation from section 8) requiring honest-irregularity practices; the over-grouping pathology — the structure-glut: the container-crutch escalation (nested boxes and cards until chrome dominates — the fragmentation cost: grouping structure consuming the ink and attention budgeted for data), the similarity-overload (every element styled into some family until no family means anything — the grouping-inflation devaluation), and the simplicity-restoration practice (the group-count budget, the strongest-cue-only discipline); the grouping-conflict diagnosis — the ambiguity failures: the bistable layouts (compositions flickering between two organizations — the unresolved-cue-conflict from section 6, the Rubin-vase dynamics at dashboard scale), the dominance-errors (the strongest cue carrying the least important grouping — the misallocated-connectedness), and the resolution workflow (identify the competing cues, reassign by importance hierarchy, verify first-parse stability) as the debugging procedure; the deceptive-design boundary — the ethics edge: grouping mechanisms weaponized (the false-proximity implication of relationship — the adjacent-bars-comparison trap, the misleading-closure of incomplete data, the cherry-picked common-region framing — the container-as-argument manipulation) with the honesty standard (grouping structure should reflect data structure, not impose preferred readings — the dark-pattern line from the behavioral-ethics tradition); the data-hygiene integration — the upstream failures: grouping artifacts from data problems (the duplicate-record clusters, the missing-value gaps misread as structure, the join-errors creating phantom categories) requiring the EDA verification (the course-11 integration — wrangle-then-see discipline: verify the pattern in data before trusting it in pixels); the hygiene-checklist synthesis — the defensive routine: the pre-publication grouping audit (the accidental-pattern scan, the illusion-context check, the aliasing inspection, the over-grouping count, the conflict-stability test, the honesty review) as the systematic defense; the failure-library practice — the institutional learning: the documented-failure catalog maintenance (the team pattern-library of grouping bugs with root-cause tags — the craft-course critique integration) making hygiene knowledge cumulative; and the hygiene deliverable — the failure-aware, audit-protected, honesty-bounded grouping practice.
