# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Deploy common fate as the dominant dynamic grouping cue with motion-detector mechanism understanding
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Gestalt perception scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** deploy common fate as the dominant dynamic grouping cue with motion-detector mechanism understanding, engineer animated transitions preserving object identity and teaching data transformations through enter-update-exit continuity, choreograph motion hierarchies (staggering, duration-easing grammar, motion budgets) directing parse order, enforce vestibular-safety constraints (reduced-motion fallbacks, flash limits) with static-equivalence validation, and prevent animation failures (continuity breaks, overload, decoration, loop fatigue) through dynamic audit routines including change-detection-informed update design.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone

**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
