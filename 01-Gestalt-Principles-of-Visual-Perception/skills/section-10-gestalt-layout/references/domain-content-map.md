# Domain Content Map — Gestalt in Layout: Dashboards, Pages, and Multi-Panel Composition

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Compose dashboard and page layouts as large-scale grouping systems with panel container hierarchies, reading-path engineering (scan patterns, top-left primacy, path-shaping cues), and alignment-grid backbones.
2. Manage information density through compensation stacks and density zoning preserving proximity grammar, bind cross-panel coordinated views through shared encodings and linked-highlight dynamics.
3. Execute responsive grouping (breakpoints at semantic boundaries) and layout-system tokenization.
4. Audit compositions through page-scale squint, five-second, and path-tracing tests preventing panel-soup, chrome-competition, and path-collapse failures.
