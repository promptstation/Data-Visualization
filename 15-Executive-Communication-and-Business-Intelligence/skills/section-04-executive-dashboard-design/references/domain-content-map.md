## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Design executive dashboards through foundations (Few-tradition definition with attention-governance reading, question-answering standard, strategic-operational-analytic taxonomy with hybrid honesty, audience-tier architecture, product lifecycle positioning), engineer information design (visual hierarchy with zone architecture and scanning paths, KPI card anatomy with consistency grammar, monitoring chart selection with restraint, grid-responsive-print layout systems, information scent through drill-down affordances and freshness indicators), master metric display craft (comparison architecture across targets-periods-peers-composition, trend communication with sparklines and forecast uncertainty, exception display with fatigue management, metric-tree drill-down pathways, honesty discipline against distortion and denominator invisibility).
2. Run production governance (question-inventory requirements through data reconciliation, implementation with semantic-layer integration and performance engineering, adoption management with usage analytics and training, governance with ownership-review-change protocols, QA covering accuracy-visual-accessibility-interpretation), and avoid christmas trees, purpose conflation, context-free tiles, traffic-light reductionism, zombie dashboards, definition divergence, screenshot illegibility, and adoption assumptions failures.
