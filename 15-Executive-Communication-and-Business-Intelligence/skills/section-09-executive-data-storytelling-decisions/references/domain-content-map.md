## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Storytell for business decisions through narrative foundations (memory-motivation-sensemaking evidence with transport ethics, business story anatomy of character-tension-resolution-moral, story type repertoire across origin-cautionary-vision-turnaround patterns, story-manipulation boundary with evidence anchoring).
2. Integrate narrative and data (story arc patterns across discovery-problem-solution-before-after forms, sequencing craft with zoom and reveal timing, representative character selection with anonymization handling, quantitative balance with statistic humanization, multi-artifact story coherence).
3. Apply venue patterns (strategy narratives with scenarios and board precision, change storytelling with burning platforms and progress episodes, performance narratives with attribution care, crisis recovery arcs, customer voice integration, personal analyst storytelling).
4. Run story production (discovery through pattern translation and audience matching, construction workflow from beats through read-aloud iteration, testing with takeaway-fact-check-bias protocols, ethical review covering harm-manipulation-completeness-consent, maintenance systems), and avoid decoration stories, exotic cases, highlight reels, retcons, emotional overrides, jargon failures, unverified anecdotes, and story monoculture failures.

**Scope:** Covers the storytelling discipline adapted from narrative visualization to executive decision contexts: the business-narrative foundations — why story works in boardrooms: the narrative-advantage evidence (the memory-and-retention findings where storied information survives meetings and relays better than bullet facts, applying the cognitive traditions from the narrative courses; the tension-structure where business complications, threats, and opportunities create the narrative drive the SCQA openings of section 2 formalize; the resolution-arc where the recommendation or decision serves as story resolution connecting narrative satisfaction to action; the moral-equivalent where the takeaway or strategic lesson carries the story's meaning into decision context); the story-types-for-business (the origin-and-journey narratives explaining how situations developed, serving investigation and context purposes; and the story-versus-manipulation boundary (the evidence-anchoring requirement where business narratives rest on verified data with the fiction distinction maintained per the honesty traditions; the selective-narrative awareness where story power enables cherry-picked protagonists and timeframes requiring the integrity audits of section 7; the audience-autonomy respect where stories inform decisions rather than bypass deliberation, applying the dark-pattern consciousness from the semiographics course) as the foundations layer; the narrative-data integration — story structures over evidence: the data-story-arc patterns (the discovery-arc where analysis journeys from question through investigation to finding serve exploratory contexts, adapting the scrollytelling narrative forms; the problem-solution-arc where situation quantification leads through driver analysis to recommendation serves decision briefs; the before-after-arc where change measurement carries improvement and impact stories with baseline honesty; the day-in-the-life patterns where operational metrics gain meaning through concrete narrative walkthroughs connecting numbers to lived reality); the narrative-sequencing craft (the chronology-versus-logic selection where story order follows either time sequence or argument sequence per the horizontal-logic distinctions of section 2; the zoom-pattern application where narrative moves between organizational scale and individual instance creating the telescoping engagement from the narrative traditions; the reveal-timing discipline where key findings land at narrative moments of maximum readiness, applying the suspense-and-payoff structures with executive-context restraint; the transition-craft between story segments maintaining flow through causal and temporal bridges); the character-and-instance selection (the representative-case discipline where story examples genuinely typify patterns rather than exotic outliers, the honesty requirement continuing section 8's anecdote cautions; the composite-and-anonymization handling where privacy and sensitivity require constructed examples with transparent labeling per section 12's ethics; the executive-relevance-matching where selected instances connect to audience experience and responsibility domains); the quantitative-narrative balance (the number-narrative-ratio management where story segments carry digestible figure loads per the cognitive-load traditions; the statistic-humanization patterns where aggregate numbers gain individual-level counterparts making magnitudes felt; the narrative-contextualization where figures receive story-embedded interpretation rather than standing bare per section 3's context discipline; the precision-narrative-tension where story smoothness must not round away decision-material details, the honesty calibration); and the multi-artifact story systems (the story-ecosystem coherence where dashboard, report, deck, and briefing versions of one narrative maintain consistency across section 1's artifact landscape; the campaign-narratives where sustained strategic stories develop across multiple communications over weeks and months; the story-version-management where evolving narratives track changes honestly without silent retconning of earlier claims) as the integration layer; the executive storytelling applications — the venue patterns: the strategy-narrative craft (the strategic-story development where organizational direction receives narrative form connecting present data to future vision per the alignment purposes; the scenario-narratives where strategic options get storied futures making abstract scenarios concrete for decision discussion, applying the scenario-planning traditions; the competitive-narratives where market position and movement receive story form with the fairness obligations of comparison honesty; the board-storytelling where governance audiences receive strategic narratives with fiduciary precision and regulatory awareness from section 6); the change-and-transformation storytelling (the burning-platform-and-vision combination where current-state data motivates and future-state narrative attracts, the change-management communication pattern; the progress-narrative systems where transformation journeys receive episodic storytelling maintaining momentum through milestones and honest setbacks; the resistance-acknowledgment craft where change stories address costs and concerns rather than pure optimism, building credibility through completeness; the culture-story-seeding where narrative examples model desired behaviors connecting to section 13's culture systems); the performance-and-results storytelling (the quarterly-narrative conventions where business reviews tell the period's story through data with the commentary disciplines of section 6; the success-and-failure-balance where honest performance stories include shortfalls with analysis rather than highlight reels only; the attribution-story care where performance narratives assign causes with the causality disciplines of section 3; the trend-narrative construction where longitudinal data becomes organizational memory and identity through repeated story threads); the crisis-and-recovery narratives (the incident-story structure where crises receive factual chronology, impact quantification, response narrative, and lesson integration per section 11's difficult communications; the recovery-arc tracking where remediation progress gets storied with milestone honesty; the post-mortem-narrative craft where failure stories serve learning with psychological-safety awareness); the customer-and-market storytelling (the voice-of-customer integration where qualitative data carries narrative authority alongside quantitative measures per section 3's qualitative balance; the market-shift-narratives where industry data becomes storied context for strategic decisions; the product-story patterns where usage data narrates customer experience with the journey-mapping traditions); and the personal-analyst-storytelling (the credibility-narrative where analysts' own discovery journeys build trust through demonstrated process transparency; the advocacy-stories where data professionals champion investments and corrections through narrative persuasion per section 1's honest-advocacy purposes; the teaching-narratives where analytical concepts reach executive audiences through story-based explanation connecting to section 13's literacy programs) as the applications layer; the storytelling production process — from data to narrative: the story-discovery method (the pattern-to-story translation where analytical findings get examined for narrative potential including characters, tension, and arc; the audience-story-matching where narrative forms align with executive preferences and organizational culture per section 1; the message-first-story-second discipline where required takeaways shape story selection rather than story appeal driving message distortion); the story-construction workflow (the outline-and-beat development where narrative structure gets sketched before content production, applying section 2's storylining to story form; the evidence-gathering-for-beats where each story moment receives supporting data with gap identification; the draft-and-read-aloud iteration where spoken rhythm and comprehension get tested per the oral traditions; the visual-story-integration where charts, images, and slides receive narrative-purpose assignment per section 7's craft); the story-testing protocols (the takeaway-test where listeners report the intended message and moral without prompting; the fact-check-pass where every narrative claim faces data verification per section 12's accuracy systems; the bias-audit where story selections face the cherry-picking and representation scrutiny of section 7's honesty layers; the audience-simulation where representative executives preview stories catching comprehension and political landmines per section 8's preparation); the ethical-storytelling review (the harm-assessment where story subjects including customers and employees face dignity and privacy review per section 12's ethics; the manipulation-check where narrative techniques face the dark-pattern audit ensuring persuasion serves informed decision; the completeness-honesty where omitted context gets evaluated for material deception risk; the source-and-consent verification where customer stories and quotes carry appropriate permissions); and the story-maintenance systems (the narrative-consistency-tracking where recurring story threads maintain coherence across communications per the ecosystem discipline; the story-retirement judgment where narratives outlive their relevance and get replaced honestly; the story-library-management where reusable narrative assets including cases, examples, and frameworks get curated for organizational use) as the process layer; and the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the decoration-story, narrative flourishes wrapping thin analysis where story substitutes for evidence, remedied by the evidence-anchoring requirement; the exotic-case-selection, cherry-picked outliers starring as representative protagonists distorting pattern understanding, remedied by the representative-case discipline and bias audits; the highlight-reel-performance, success-only narratives omitting shortfalls until credibility collapses on discovery, remedied by the success-failure balance; the retcon-narrative, silently revising earlier story claims when facts evolve, remedied by the version-management and honest-evolution practices; the emotional-override, story transport used to bypass deliberation on consequential decisions, remedied by the audience-autonomy respect and manipulation checks; the jargon-story-failure, narratives collapsing into technical exposition losing story engagement, remedied by the character-tension-resolution anatomy discipline; the unverified-anecdote, compelling customer or employee stories circulating without fact-checking until challenged, remedied by the testing protocols and source verification; and the story-monoculture, all communications forced into narrative form where direct analysis would serve better, remedied by the venue-matching judgment and message-first discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.
