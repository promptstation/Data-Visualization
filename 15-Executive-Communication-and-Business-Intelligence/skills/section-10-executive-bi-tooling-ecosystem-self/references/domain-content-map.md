## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Master the BI tooling ecosystem through landscape literacy (platform categories across enterprise-semantic-code-first-lightweight-spreadsheet tiers, selection frameworks with total cost and proof of concept, specialization patterns with portfolio governance, AI and augmented analytics frontier awareness with skepticism, vendor relationship management).
2. Build self-service programs (autonomy spectrum concept with enablement-governance balance, business case with failure-mode awareness and readiness assessment, certified-exploratory architecture with semantic foundations, tiered support with champion networks, gradual rollout discipline), craft training and enablement (literacy program design with role-based paths and real data, workflow-based tool training with quality standards and certification, documentation craft with metric dictionaries, community building, program measurement), govern at scale (standards publication with certification marking, automated quality checks and sampling reviews, access-lifecycle-ownership governance, metric governance integration, proportionality and friction design), evolve specialist practice (consulting patterns with design reviews, platform craft with component libraries and automation, influence without authority, career development toward decision infrastructure, burnout-boundary management), and avoid tool-first fallacies, ungoverned dumps, feature tours, certification theater, bottleneck persistence, spreadsheet dismissal, template monoculture, and frontier hype failures.
