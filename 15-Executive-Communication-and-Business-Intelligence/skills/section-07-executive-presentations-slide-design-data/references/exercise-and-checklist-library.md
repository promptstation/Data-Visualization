# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Build executive presentations through medium foundations (deck role distinct from documents with afterlife obligations
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Executive communication and business intelligence scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** build executive presentations through medium foundations (deck role distinct from documents with afterlife obligations, format taxonomy across live-pre-read-leave-behind-standing-remote, cognitive bases in dual-channel processing and redundancy effects, presentation ethics with cognitive-style critique awareness), architect decks (title-as-conclusion openings with agenda roadmaps, action titles passing storyline tests, SCQA-journey-parallel-contrast flow patterns, time-pacing architecture with attention arcs and buffers, deck family consistency with template governance), craft slides (grid layouts with one-idea discipline, chart-dominant data slides with build restraint, text minimalism with size floors, visual support elements, accessibility standards, content-then-design workflows), enforce numbers-on-slides honesty (scale standards with cross-slide consistency, rounding-materiality-precision communication, comparison integrity against cherry picking, uncertainty representation, source and as-of transparency), and avoid bullet walls, topic titles, format confusion, pacing fantasy, chart junk, scale sleight, appendix absence, and template anarchy failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.
