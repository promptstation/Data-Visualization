# Domain Content Map — Executive Reports and Written Communication: Memos, One-Pagers, and Board Materials

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Produce executive written artifacts through landscape literacy (memo-one-pager-report-board-email taxonomy, form selection by decision context and culture, scan-first multi-device reading premises), craft analytical memos (conclusion-carrying architecture, narrative memo traditions including six-page culture, reasoning display with assumptions-alternatives-uncertainty-counterarguments, exhibit integration with verification.
2. Review processes), master short forms (one-pager anatomy with standalone tests, status report conventions with variance obligations, decision briefs with options-tradeoffs-approval mechanics, email craft with subject-opening-inline-data discipline, exception and progress messages).
3. Run periodic reporting systems (calendar architecture with cadence hierarchy, business review pack construction with single-story requirements, monthly-quarterly-board traditions, commentary craft with attribution honesty, automation with QA, portfolio retirement), enforce production quality (editorial and style standards, visual robustness with template governance, document management hygiene, legal compliance awareness, accessibility obligations), and avoid topic subjects, deck-memos, forwarded fragments, report zombies, commentary restatement, contradictory packs, unverified numbers, and inaccessible formality failures.
