# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Deliver live data communication through briefing foundations (real-time bidirectional distinctiveness with presence dynamics
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Executive communication and business intelligence scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** deliver live data communication through briefing foundations (real-time bidirectional distinctiveness with presence dynamics, format taxonomy across formal-conversational-standup-walkthrough-one-on-one, preparation with key-message definition and material mastery, rehearsal protocols with opening-closing precision), speak numbers fluently (spoken rounding with scale anchoring and pacing, verbal chart narration with synchronization and takeaways, explanation vocabulary covering jargon-uncertainty-causality-caveats, storytelling devices with representativeness honesty, listening-adaptation skills), master Q&A discipline (question-type taxonomy, direct-answer architecture with bridges and length discipline, defense protocols for challenged numbers and logic with unknown-answer honesty, hostile-question management, facilitation skills, integration design with anticipation and follow-up), navigate meeting dynamics (stakeholder alignment and coalition awareness, meeting flow management with decision capture, remote-hybrid adaptation, difficult conversation protocols, relationship building through consistency), and avoid unprepared improvisation, jargon floods, defensive unknowns, rambling, loaded-question acceptance, live surprises, correction spirals, and relay distortion failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
