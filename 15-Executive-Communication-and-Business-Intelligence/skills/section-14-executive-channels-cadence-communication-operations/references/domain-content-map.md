## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Operate communication distribution through channel landscape mastery (taxonomy across synchronous-asynchronous-notification-conversational-broadcast, richness-record-reach-formality characteristic analysis, purpose-audience-urgency selection logic, portfolio management with retirement discipline, cross-channel consistency with contradiction detection), architect cadence (nested rhythm hierarchy with collision management, periodic cycle craft across daily-weekly-monthly-quarterly-annual, event-driven triggers with incident rhythms, audience-specific cadences including executive-board-organization patterns, quality management against drift).
2. Run communication operations (production with editorial calendars and automation, distribution with verification and timing, feedback and listening loops, crisis surge protocols, tools-platform operations, measurement systems).
3. Adapt to distributed contexts (async-first principles with timezone engineering, digital channel mastery across chat-video-collaboration-mobile, overload management with signal-noise engineering, cross-cultural global dimensions, emerging channel frontier evaluation), and avoid channel sprawl, cadence drift, async failures, alert floods, single-channel dependencies, surge improvisation, overload normalization, and frontier fads failures.
