# Domain Content Map — The Structured Business Argument: Pyramid Principle, SCQA, and Answer-First Communication

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Structure business arguments through the pyramid principle (answer-first doctrine with hierarchical idea structure, vertical question-answer dialogue, horizontal deductive-inductive logic, MECE grouping discipline with size limits and issue-tree application, SCQA openings, pyramid testing protocols), craft executive summaries (recommendation-first anatomy with ask-and-next-steps, standalone completeness with claim-evidence density and calibrated language, artifact family consistency with version synchronization, cold-reader and scan testing).
2. Run argument construction (message mapping with objection anticipation, action-title storylining with continuity checks, analysis-argument sequencing with negative-results honesty and sufficiency judgment, structure selection by context), master business writing mechanics (plain language with jargon translation and acronym governance, paragraph architecture with topic sentences and white space, number presentation with rounding-context-integration rules, document navigation systems, reverse-outline editing discipline), and avoid buried ledes, logic gaps, MECE violations, teaser summaries, action-title absence, structure inversion, jargon walls, and bare numbers failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the argument architecture that converts analysis into executive-consumable structure: the pyramid principle foundation — the Minto tradition: the core architecture (the answer-first doctrine where the central recommendation or conclusion opens the communication and all supporting material organizes beneath it, inverting the academic and investigative build-up sequences that bury conclusions; the hierarchical idea structure where every point summarizes the ideas grouped below it, creating the pyramid of abstractions readable at any depth; the vertical question-answer dialogue where each point raises questions in the reader's mind that the level below answers, propelling comprehension; the horizontal logic where ideas at each level form deductive chains or inductive groupings with explicit relational structure); the MECE grouping discipline (the mutually-exclusive-collectively-exhaustive standard for grouping supporting arguments, preventing overlap gaps and redundancy; the grouping-by-category-structure-time-and-importance patterns as the standard logical orderings; the grouping-size discipline where three-to-five supporting arguments per level match working-memory capacity from the cognitive traditions; the MECE application to analysis itself where issue trees decompose business questions exhaustively before data work begins, connecting to the consulting problem-solving traditions); the SCQA opening structure (the Situation-Complication-Question-Answer sequence establishing context, tension, and resolution in the first minutes or paragraphs; the situation-as-agreed-truth convention opening with what the audience already accepts, building common ground; the complication-as-change device introducing the development that demands response, creating narrative tension from the storytelling traditions; the question-answer pivot where the complication implies the question the communication then answers directly); the deductive versus inductive reasoning structures (the deductive chain of major-premise-minor-premise-conclusion with its power and fragility where challenging any premise collapses the argument; the inductive grouping of parallel supporting evidence with its robustness and the synthesis demand; the executive-preference findings where inductive structures typically serve decision audiences better than deductive chains, reserving deduction for specific persuasive contexts); and the pyramid testing protocols (the so-what interrogation where every supporting point must earn its place by answering why it matters to the conclusion; the summary-only test where reading just the top levels must convey the complete argument skeleton; the question-trace verification where each level genuinely answers the questions the level above raises) as the pyramid layer; the executive summary craft — the compression discipline: the summary anatomy (the recommendation-or-conclusion first sentence delivering the answer without preamble, the BLUF bottom-line-up-front convention from the professional communication traditions; the key-supporting-points sentence group covering the two-to-four load-bearing arguments; the ask-and-next-steps closing specifying what decision or action is requested with timing; the length discipline where executive summaries hold one page or less regardless of underlying analysis depth); the summary-writing craft (the standalone-completeness standard where the summary works for readers who never open the body, requiring self-contained context; the claim-evidence density where every sentence carries either a conclusion or its strongest support, eliminating transitional and methodological filler; the number-selection judgment where the two-or-three most decision-relevant figures appear while supporting statistics stay in the body; the language-calibration matching confidence phrasing to evidence strength per the uncertainty conventions of section 11); the summary-artifact relationships (the summary-versus-abstract distinction where abstracts describe content while executive summaries deliver arguments and recommendations; the document-family consistency where summaries, deck openings, dashboard callouts, and email versions tell one story at different resolutions; the summary-updating discipline where conclusions evolve through analysis with all summary versions synchronized, the version-control challenge from the systems traditions); and the summary-testing protocols (the cold-reader test where uninformed colleagues extract the recommendation, key support, and ask correctly; the executive-simulation reading where the summary faces the actual thirty-second scan pattern; the ask-clarity verification where the requested action is unambiguous in scope, owner, and timing) as the summary layer; the argument construction process — from analysis to structure: the message-map development (the key-message identification asking what the audience must understand, believe, and do after the communication; the supporting-evidence inventory matching available analysis to each key message with gap identification driving further analysis; the objection-and-question anticipation listing likely executive challenges with prepared responses, connecting to the Q&A discipline of section 8; the narrative-line selection choosing the ordering logic that best serves the audience's reasoning path); the storylining practice (the slide-or-section-title-as-argument-sentence convention where titles alone read as the complete argument sequence, the action-title discipline from the consulting traditions; the storyline-review protocol where the title sequence faces logical-flow and completeness testing before any content production; the horizontal-continuity check ensuring each section's content delivers what its title promises; the vertical-depth calibration where supporting detail matches each point's importance and contestability); the analysis-argument relationship (the analysis-first-then-structure sequence where data work precedes message design, guarding against conclusion-driven analysis from the honesty traditions; the iterative-refinement reality where emerging findings reshape storylines through documented versions rather than silent revision; the negative-results handling where analyses contradicting the working hypothesis get integrated honestly, applying the counter-evidence treatment from the rhetoric traditions of the semiographics course; the sufficiency-judgment where analysis stops when decision-relevant uncertainty is resolved rather than when curiosity is exhausted, the diminishing-returns discipline); and the structure-selection-by-context (the recommendation-brief structure for decision requests; the status-report structure for monitoring contexts with exception-emphasis; the investigation-narrative structure for exploratory findings where the discovery journey serves comprehension; the option-analysis structure for comparative decisions with explicit tradeoff matrices) as the construction layer; the business writing mechanics — sentence and paragraph craft: the plain-language discipline (the jargon-translation obligation converting technical and analytical vocabulary into business language with defined-terms-on-first-use conventions; the sentence-length management where executive materials favor short declarative sentences carrying single ideas; the active-voice-and-agent-clarity standard where who-did-what remains unambiguous, critical for accountability contexts; the acronym governance where organizational abbreviation culture is navigated with expansion discipline for cross-functional audiences); the paragraph architecture (the topic-sentence-first convention where each paragraph announces its point before developing it, the paragraph-level pyramid; the one-idea-per-paragraph discipline with paragraph length as a scanning signal; the transition-craft connecting paragraphs through logical consequence rather than decorative bridges; the white-space-as-structure principle where visual paragraph formatting supports scanning from the typography traditions of the semiographics course); the number-presentation mechanics (the rounding-and-precision conventions matching decision relevance where millions-level decisions do not need thousands-level precision; the unit-and-denominator clarity standards preventing scale misreading; the comparison-mandatory rule where numbers never appear bare, always with baseline, target, trend, or peer context per the metric-context discipline of section 3; the number-narrative integration where figures serve sentences making claims rather than floating as decoration); the document-navigation systems (the heading-hierarchy design supporting scan-reading with informative rather than generic headings; the callout-and-sidebar conventions highlighting key figures and implications for scanners; the exhibit-numbering-and-referencing discipline where charts and tables integrate with text argument per the anchorage traditions; the appendix-architecture where methodology, detail, and caveats live accessibly without interrupting the main argument); and the editing-and-revision practice (the reverse-outline audit extracting headings and topic sentences to test structural logic post-draft; the cut-ruthlessly discipline where every element faces deletion testing against the communication purpose; the peer-review-before-executive-review pipeline catching structural and factual problems at appropriate cost levels) as the mechanics layer; and the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the buried lede, opening with background and methodology while executives wait for the point, remedied by the answer-first doctrine and SCQA openings; the logic-gapse, pyramid levels that fail to answer the questions raised above them, remedied by the vertical question-answer testing; the MECE violation, overlapping or missing supporting arguments leaving the conclusion underdefended, remedied by the grouping discipline and so-what interrogation; the summary-as-teaser, executive summaries that describe content without delivering conclusions, remedied by the standalone-completeness standard; the action-title-absence, slide and section titles naming topics instead of stating arguments, remedied by the storylining practice; the analysis-before-structure inversion, designing messages before data supports them, remedied by the analysis-first sequencing with documented iteration; the jargon-wall, technical vocabulary excluding the decision audience, remedied by the plain-language discipline; and the bare-number scatter, figures without comparison context forcing executives to guess at meaning, remedied by the comparison-mandatory rule with detection methods as the diagnostic.
