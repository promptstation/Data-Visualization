# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Communicate uncertainty and difficulty through foundations (uncertainty taxonomy across data-statistical-model-deep categories
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Executive communication and business intelligence scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** communicate uncertainty and difficulty through foundations (uncertainty taxonomy across data-statistical-model-deep categories, quantification literacy with intervals and probability conventions, calibration discipline against overconfidence, uncertainty attitude spectrum with false-precision resistance, documentation systems with decision-relevance filtering), craft risk communication (framing systems with calibrated language and appetite context, visualization patterns from heatmaps to tail risks, narrative construction with mitigation clarity, perception management navigating biases ethically, cadence across standing-escalation-emerging-review), deliver bad news (typology across shortfalls-findings-failures-deterioration, delivery protocols with early escalation and response integration, psychological-political navigation of messenger risk and sunk costs, organizational learning through blameless post-mortems, recovery narrative management).
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.
