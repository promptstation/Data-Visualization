the MECE application to analysis itself where issue trees decompose business questions exhaustively before data work begins, connecting to the consulting problem-solving traditions); the SCQA opening structure (the Situation-Complication-Question-Answer sequence establishing context, tension, and resolution in the first minutes or paragraphs; the question-answer pivot where the complication implies the question the communication then answers directly); the deductive versus inductive reasoning structures (the deductive chain of major-premise-minor-premise-conclusion with its power and fragility where challenging any premise collapses the argument; and the pyramid testing protocols (the so-what interrogation where every supporting point must earn its place by answering why it matters to the conclusion; the summary-only test where reading just the top levels must convey the complete argument skeleton; the summary-artifact relationships (the summary-versus-abstract distinction where abstracts describe content while executive summaries deliver arguments and recommendations; the document-family consistency where summaries, deck openings, dashboard callouts, and email versions tell one story at different resolutions; the narrative-line selection choosing the ordering logic that best serves the audience's reasoning path); the storylining practice (the slide-or-section-title-as-argument-sentence convention where titles alone read as the complete argument sequence, the action-title discipline from the consulting traditions; the vertical-depth calibration where supporting detail matches each point's importance and contestability); the analysis-argument relationship (the analysis-first-then-structure sequence where data work precedes message design, guarding against conclusion-driven analysis from the honesty traditions;
