## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Build semiotic design systems through the visual language concept (vocabulary-syntax-semantics framing with consistency-as-code-building principle.
2. Design system lineage with visualization-specific layers, scope definition across artifacts-audiences-boundaries).
3. Construct the vocabulary layer (encoding convention registries with chart type palettes and honesty rules, color system governance with functional-semantic-theme architectures, icon-symbol libraries, typography systems with numeric and expressive standards, annotation-narrative vocabularies with metaphor-register guidance), codify the syntax layer (composition grammar with layout-hierarchy-density rules, multi-view coherence and interaction semantics, narrative composition with anchorage standards, responsive-dialect-medium adaptation rules, exception governance with invention sandboxes).
4. Operate the system (meaning maintenance with semantic changelogs and collision detection, contribution-review workflows with semiotic checks, adoption-comprehension-consistency measurement, documentation-education-tooling support, evolution strategy with research integration and federation).
5. Execute the building journey (audit-baseline phase with quick wins, foundation construction with descriptive-then-prescriptive sequencing, rollout with migration-tooling-support, maturity sustainment with governance institutionalization, scale-context adaptation from lightweight teams to enterprise federation), and avoid vocabulary-without-semantics, ivory towers, exception erosion, suffocation, undocumented rationale, tooling absence, frozen languages.
6. Audit omission failures.

the consistency-suffocation, enforcing uniformity where dialect variation serves audiences better, remedied by the boundary and dialect architecture; the undocumented-rationale, shipping rules without the reasoning that makes them defensible and adaptable, remedied by the decision documentation requirements; and the audit-omission, building systems without baseline measurement so improvement remains unproven, remedied by the measurement and health monitoring practices with detection methods as the diagnostic.
