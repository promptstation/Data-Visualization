3. Run production workflows (brief-to-metaphor process with concept testing, mapping fidelity engineering with channel preservation and pictogram scaling honesty, anchorage-legend integration with progressive disclosure, quality gates covering clash-connotation-accessibility-accuracy), extend to dynamic metaphors (direct manipulation and navigation patterns, transformation storytelling with transition coherence, mixed reality awareness, dynamic risk management).
4. Apply domain patterns (journalism explanatory and satirical graphics, business KPI conventions and executive framing, science-health mechanism and risk communication with uncertainty hybrids, education scaffold-transfer assessment), and avoid unmapped decoration, pictogram scaling errors, physics violations, monoculture, engagement bait, interaction clash, and audience blindness failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the applied craft of building visual metaphors that communicate rather than merely decorate: the infographic tradition — the applied metaphor ecosystem: the infographic form and history (the illustrated-information tradition from the Isotype pictorial statistics of section 7's preview through mid-century instructional graphics to the digital infographic boom of the 2010s, where metaphor-rich presentation became a mass format; the infographic taxonomy where data-rich explainer graphics, statistical posters, and narrative visual essays differ in metaphor density and evidence obligations; the viral-infographic economy where shareability incentives drove both innovation and the decoration-over-data failures documented in the critique literature); the metaphor-forward infographic patterns (the object-substitution pattern where the entire graphic takes a source-domain shape, such as a budget rendered as a pie of food, importing container and consumption entailments; the scene-construction pattern where data inhabits a depicted environment like a factory, city, or body, importing systemic process entailments; the icon-array scaling pattern where quantities appear as repeated pictorial units, importing countability and human-scale empathy; the transformation pattern where data flows through depicted mechanisms like pipes, funnels, and machines, importing process and conservation logic from the physical metaphor family of section 5); the infographic critique and defense (the accuracy-sacrifice findings where metaphor-forward designs measurably reduce value extraction relative to standard charts in controlled studies, the core evidence behind chartjunk controversies analyzed in section 13; the engagement and memory counter-evidence where metaphorical infographics increase attention, sharing, and recall for audiences that standard charts lose; the audience-fit resolution where metaphor density should match audience data literacy, context of consumption, and communication purpose rather than designer taste, from the effectiveness moderators of section 5); and the professional practice standards (the editorial infographic workflow where metaphor concepts pass data verification before illustration, from the journalism traditions; the source and method transparency conventions even in stylized graphics; and the hybrid designs where metaphor framing wraps conventionally encoded data cores, capturing engagement while preserving accuracy) as the infographic layer; the metaphorical chart families — the hybrid instruments: the gauge and meter family (the speedometer metaphor importing rate, danger zones, and single-value focus, with its dashboard-etymology from automobile instrumentation; the bullet graph redesign as the semiotically disciplined alternative stripping the gauge metaphor while keeping threshold zones, from the Tufte tradition analyzed in section 13; the appropriate and inappropriate gauge contexts where single-metric status communication suits the form while multi-value comparison does not); the physical-process chart family (the funnel importing flow-narrowing and conversion-stage logic with its volume-conservation entailments that data often violates; the Sankey importing fluid conservation where widths sum through splits, a metaphor whose entailment the form actually enforces; the waterfall importing cumulative rise-fall with gravity and level connotations; and the streamgraph importing river flow with its layered continuity aesthetics and baseline-sensitivity cautions from the charting traditions); the landscape and terrain family (the heatmap importing temperature intensity with hotspot connotations analyzed in section 5's families; the contour and surface plots importing topography with elevation-as-magnitude mappings; the treemap importing nested territory and land-division entailments where area means quantity; and the volcano and seismograph plots importing geological event magnitudes in scientific visualization conventions); the organism and population family (the phylogenetic and dendrogram trees importing evolutionary descent and branching time, where the biological metaphor's entailments license and mislead in non-biological hierarchies; the ecosystem and network-of-life framings in environmental graphics; the population pyramid importing architectural stability and balance with its age-structure reading conventions); and the family selection discipline (the entailment-audit application where each candidate chart family's metaphor is checked against whether its licensed inferences match the data's actual behavior, operationalizing section 5's critique method; the convention-maturity gradient where gauges and funnels are so conventionalized that readers decode them without active metaphor processing, reducing both power and risk; and the invention boundary where novel metaphorical charts require the comprehension testing that conventional forms can skip) as the hybrid layer; the metaphor design workflow — the production craft: the brief-to-metaphor process (the communication goal extraction identifying what understanding, feeling, and action the graphic should produce, from the audience-fit logic; the source-domain brainstorming against the data's relational structure using the structural fit analysis of section 5; the metaphor concept sketching where mapping tables become rough visual drafts before production investment; and the concept testing checkpoints where sketches face audience comprehension checks before illustration begins); the mapping fidelity engineering (the systematic correspondence maintenance where every visual element in the source shape carries data meaning, eliminating unmapped decoration, from the mapping completeness principle; the quantitative channel preservation where accurate encodings survive metaphorical styling, using position, length, and area correctly inside illustrated forms, from the channel disciplines of sections 3 and 4; the scale and proportion honesty audits where pictorial scaling follows the area-volume perception rules preventing the classic pictogram doubling errors where doubled height quadruples perceived size, from the psychophysics traditions); the anchorage and legend integration (the title-and-caption anchoring that fixes the metaphor reading, from the Barthes tradition of section 2; the hybrid legend design explaining both the metaphor mapping and the data encoding for novel forms; the progressive disclosure layering where casual readers get the metaphor gestalt and motivated readers access precise values through interaction or detail panels, from the disclosure traditions); and the production quality gates (the metaphor-clash review checking source-target logic consistency from section 5's failure modes; the connotation review screening cultural and emotional associations of the chosen source across target audiences, previewing sections 9 and 10; the accessibility review ensuring metaphor-dependent meaning has text and table equivalents from the accessibility traditions; and the accuracy benchmarking comparing value extraction against a conventional chart baseline to quantify the metaphor's precision cost) as the workflow layer; the interactive and animated metaphor — the dynamic craft: the interactive metaphor patterns (the direct manipulation metaphors where dragging, zooming, and filtering import physical object handling, from the interface metaphor traditions; the exploration metaphors of navigation where maps, spaces, and journeys structure data exploration, connecting to the information architecture mantra traditions; the manipulation feedback as metaphor maintenance where responsive animations sustain the physical illusion, connecting to the narrative-course motion craft); the animated metaphor sequences (the transformation storytelling where morphing between metaphor states shows data change through source-domain events, such as water levels rising or populations growing, importing process and causality entailments; the transition coherence requirement where animated metaphor changes preserve object identity and mapping continuity so blends survive the motion, from the object permanence traditions; the duration and pacing as rhetorical control where animation speed imports urgency and importance readings); the mixed-reality extensions at awareness (the spatial computing and VR data environments where users inhabit data metaphors with embodiment effects on understanding from the immersive visualization research; the augmented reality overlays blending physical and data spaces with their registration and occlusion semantics); the dynamic metaphor risks (the interaction-clash failure where manipulation behaviors contradict the active metaphor, breaking the blend; the motion-sickness and cognitive-load costs of immersive and heavy metaphor systems requiring the restraint principles from the accessibility traditions; and the state-visibility problem where interactive metaphor states diverge from data states without clear signaling, demanding the feedback sign discipline of section 2) as the dynamic layer; the domain application patterns — the field practices: the journalism and editorial practice (the explanatory metaphor graphic for complex systems like budgets, epidemics, and elections where audience breadth justifies metaphor density, from the newsroom traditions; the data-in-context scenes embedding statistics in depicted real-world situations for human-impact stories; the satire and critique graphics where metaphor carries argument and commentary, previewing section 11's rhetorical traditions); the business and dashboard practice (the KPI metaphor conventions of gauges, traffic lights, and scorecards where organizational culture has conventionalized the forms despite their semiotic poverty, analyzed critically in section 13; the executive-summary visual metaphors framing strategic situations as battles, journeys, and machines with their entailment consequences for decision thinking; the process-mapping metaphors where value streams, funnels, and pipelines structure operational understanding); the science and health communication practice (the mechanism-of-action illustrations where biological and physical metaphors explain processes to patients and publics, from the health communication traditions; the risk communication metaphors where probability framing through frequencies, icons, and natural scenes affects protective behavior, with the unit chart empathy advantages from the population family; the uncertainty representation challenges where metaphor sources resist expressing probability and confidence, requiring hybrid anchorage solutions); and the education and museum practice (the instructional metaphor sequences building conceptual understanding through consistent source domains across lessons, applying the blend-running dynamics; the exhibit-scale immersive metaphors where visitors walk inside data representations; and the learning assessment integration checking whether the metaphor scaffold transfers to abstract understanding or becomes a crutch, from the learning sciences traditions) as the domain layer; and the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the unmapped decoration, illustrated source imagery carrying no data correspondence, remedied by the mapping fidelity engineering; the pictogram scaling error, doubling icon height for double quantity and quadrupling perceived area, remedied by the area-volume psychophysics audits; the funnel-physics violation, applying conservation-entailing flow metaphors to non-conserving data without annotation, remedied by the entailment audit discipline; the metaphor-monoculture, defaulting to gauges and funnels because convention removed all thought, remedied by the convention-maturity awareness and family selection reasoning; the engagement-bait infographic, maximizing shareability through decorative spectacle while data accuracy collapses, remedied by the accuracy benchmarking gates; the interaction-clash, building manipulations contradicting the displayed metaphor, remedied by the blend consistency review; and the audience-blindness, calibrating metaphor density to designer taste rather than audience literacy and context, remedied by the audience-fit resolution framework with detection methods as the diagnostic.
