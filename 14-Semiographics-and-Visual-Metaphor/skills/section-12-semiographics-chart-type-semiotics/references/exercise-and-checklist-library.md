# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Read and select chart types semiotically through the analysis framework (type-as-sign-system concept with convention archaeology
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Semiographics and visual metaphor scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** read and select chart types semiotically through the analysis framework (type-as-sign-system concept with convention archaeology, encoding-metaphor-reading-genre-failure analysis dimensions, selection as meaning decision), master the comparison family (bar encoding with zero-baseline obligations and stacking tradeoffs, dot-lollipop-slope variants with comparison-made-visible logic, family selection by task and register), navigate proportion families (pie critique-defense balance with entailment package, treemap container semantics with color overload problems, waffle-unit forms with empathy and precision tradeoffs, radial variants, family selection with audience convention matching), command relationship-distribution families (scatter contract and enhancement warrant obligations, line continuity-interpolation entailments with banking and multi-series semantics, histogram construction reality with box-violin variants, question-to-form mapping with entailment compatibility), handle structural forms (Sankey conservation enforcement, network layout-semantics confusion with density strategies, choropleth area bias with normalization, specialized genre forms with literacy demands, repertoire synthesis through selection protocols and innovation boundaries), and avoid type-by-habit, many-part pies, truncated bars, line-over-gaps, bin manipulation, hairball acceptance, area bias, and entailment collision failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.
