# Domain Content Map — Fatigue, Sustained Attention, and the Time-on-Task Dimension

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Distinguish acute per-task load from chronic session accumulation designing for the informed-without-fatiguing dual requirement, counter vigilance decay and executive-depletion through session pacing, task variation, breakpoints, and calm-state views, govern interruption and alert economies against cumulative residue costs with breakpoint-aligned delivery.
2. Apply perceptual-ergonomics standards (luminance, typography endurance, motion calm, saturation limits) for sustained viewing.
3. Measure fatigue through longitudinal performance curves and production time-on-shift signals with expertise interaction awareness.
4. Design recovery supports (resume summaries, digests, handoff artifacts) within organizational workload advocacy preventing vigilance-assumption, interruption-flood, endurance-blind, and measurement-absence failures.
