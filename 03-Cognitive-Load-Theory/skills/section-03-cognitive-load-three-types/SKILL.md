# The Three Load Types: Intrinsic, Extraneous, Germane — Professional Training

## Mission

Produce professional-grade learning modules and training materials on **The Three Load Types: Intrinsic, Extraneous, Germane** within The science of working-memory limits applied to information design — managing intrinsic, extraneous, and germane load so complex dashboards inform users without overwhelming or fatiguing them.

Subject scope: Covers the framework's accounting core: intrinsic load — the inherent complexity: the element-interactivity of the content itself (the multi-variable comparison, the trend-exception correlation, the cross-panel integration — the interacting elements the task requires holding simultaneously), its sources in data (the dimensionality, the relationship complexity, the domain-knowledge prerequisites) and task (the comparison depth, the precision requirements), and its management impossibility-plus-alternatives (intrinsic load cannot be deleted without changing the task — it can be sequenced: the part-task progressions, the completed-example scaffolds, the isolation-of-elements-then-integration pedagogy applied to dashboard onboarding and layering); extraneous load — the presentation waste: the processing imposed by how information is delivered rather than what it conveys (the documented sources: split-attention layouts, redundant duplication, unclear encodings requiring conscious decoding, decorative elements demanding suppression, poor navigation requiring re-orientation) as the designer's primary responsibility — the load that buys the viewer nothing and the primary target of elimination; germane load — the productive effort: the capacity spent building and automating schemas (the sense-making work: relating new data to known patterns, constructing the mental model of the system being monitored, learning the dashboard's conventions), its status in the modern framework (the germane-as-schema-construction view — effort that is intrinsic load directed at learning), and its design treatment (protection and cultivation — the scaffolds, comparisons, and annotations that make productive thinking easier rather than eliminating all effort); the load-budget equation — the accounting practice: total load as intrinsic plus extraneous constrained by capacity, with germane load as the intrinsic-plus investment (the budget allocation: minimize extraneous → free capacity → the freed capacity funds germane processing and task performance — the load-management sequence) making every design decision a budget reallocation; the interaction dynamics — the load couplings: the documented interactions (high extraneous load crowding out germane processing — the learning-degradation under bad design; high intrinsic load amplified by extraneous additions — the overload cliff where total exceeds capacity producing the comprehension collapse; the expertise-mediated shifts — what is germane for novices becomes automated and what is extraneous for experts may be germane scaffolding for novices — the expertise-reversal preview) as the dynamic accounting; measuring the three types — the assessment mapping: the instrumentation per load type (the intrinsic: task-difficulty baselines and element-interactivity counts; the extraneous: the design-variant comparisons — same task and data, different presentation, with performance and workload deltas; the germane: the learning and transfer measures — the schema-acquisition tests, the post-exposure performance improvement) as the three-type evidence system; the dashboard-application frame — the BI translation: intrinsic load sources in dashboards (the KPI interdependencies, the time-series comparison demands, the exception-judgment complexity), extraneous load sources (the chrome, inconsistent encodings, separated labels, gratuitous animation), germane load value (the analyst's system-model building, the executive's metric-relationship learning) with the audit practice (the load-source inventory per display element — what load does this add, of which type, earning what?) as the applied accounting; the load-type anti-patterns — the failure catalog: the extraneous-as-intrinsic misdiagnosis (blaming the data's complexity when the presentation causes the strain — the fixable-problem denial), the germane-elimination error (over-simplifying until no understanding is built — the pre-chewed-dashboard dependency trap from the pitfalls), the intrinsic-avoidance (refusing the necessary complexity — the dumbing-down failure where the task genuinely requires multi-element integration), and the load-shifting illusion (moving load between views without reducing it — the progressive-disclosure-without-coherence failure previewed for section 9); the sequencing-and-scaffolding craft — the intrinsic management: the element-isolation sequences (the single-series-first then comparison layering), the worked-example progression (the annotated exemplar analyses teaching the reading patterns), and the completion-fading scaffolds (the gradually removed supports — the training-wheel cadence) as the intrinsic-load temporal management toolkit; the load-personalization horizon — the adaptive view: the expertise-conditional load profiles (the same display carrying different load compositions for different viewers — the section-14 adaptation preview) with the practical responses (the role-based views, the toggleable scaffolds, the progressive complexity) as the multi-audience load strategy; and the three-load deliverable — the accounting-fluent load classification, budgeting, and measurement practice.

Write as an experienced practitioner, not as a summarizer of popular content. Every framework taught must be something a real team or professional could run: procedures they can execute, criteria they can judge with, and artifacts they can hand to a colleague. Do not present claims as settled when the field treats them as contested — the training must model evidence discipline.

The module deepens this section's capabilities for a learner progressing from competent beginner toward expert practitioner, and connects them to the surrounding discipline rather than teaching them in isolation.

## Writing Standards

Throughout the material, prioritize language that is:

* Precise without becoming jargon-heavy
* Practical without discarding rigor
* Honest about limitations and contested findings without being defeatist
* Concrete — anchored in real cases, real artifacts, and verifiable numbers
* Progressive from fundamentals to expert judgment

Where a recommendation depends on context, explain the trade-off rather than presenting an absolute rule.

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid: Treating load as only-about-quantity — element interactivity, not element count, drives intrinsic load: four interacting series can cost more than forty parallel ones; Optimizing extraneous load to zero while ignoring germane load — frictionless displays that teach nothing, leaving users permanently dependent on the designer's pre-chewing; Expertise-reversal blindness — scaffolding that helps novices (guides, annotations, step-by-step layering) becoming redundant load for experts, one design serving neither; Density panic — stripping legitimate information to reduce felt complexity, when the real fix is structure: chunking, alignment, and progressive disclosure carrying density at low load; Measuring load by asking designers — self-report from the display's authors is worthless; load evidence requires naive users, workload scales, and performance triangulation; exercises without evaluation criteria; modules that stop at inspiration without a single decision the learner can now make better; and any content that overstates certainty beyond what the evidence supports.

## Decision Heuristic

When allocating depth under time or length limits, ask:

1. Where will this audience actually stall in practice?
2. Which unit protects the most value if taught well?
3. Can the learner run the framework tomorrow without me?
4. Is every example doing work a plain sentence could not?
5. What would a skeptical domain expert say about this material?

If two topics compete for space, keep the one that changes a decision.

The module must function simultaneously as a learning resource, a practical reference, and a working methodology.
