## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Master practical transformations through the taxonomy (rigid-affine-projective classes with platform support and data-versus-view distinctions).
2. Execute translations (vector-addition mechanics, platform patterns, data-centering, animation lerp), rotations (arbitrary-point composition, direction-angle conventions, 3D elemental matrices with Euler-quaternion awareness.
3. Map-scene-label-PCA applications), and scalings (diagonal mechanics, scale-function-as-matrix readings, stroke-text pitfalls, responsive viewBox patterns), assemble compositions (right-to-left order discipline, scene-graph hierarchies with local-world spaces, decomposition-interpolation quality craft, basis-vector debugging toolkit), bridge to homogeneous coordinates (translation-augmentation insight, w-component preview, SVG-CSS-WebGL matrix forms).
4. Integrate into pipelines (model-view-projection chains, data-to-screen scale-translate layouts, inverse-pipeline picking, GPU vertex-transform parallelism) while avoiding order-blindness, origin-only-rotation, sign-confusion, scaling-side-effects, interpolation-artifacts, stack-leaks, and unprojection failures.
