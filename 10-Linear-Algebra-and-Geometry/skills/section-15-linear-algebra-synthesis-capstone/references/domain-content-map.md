## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Synthesize the complete geometric pipeline by tracing data-to-pixels through all fourteen preceding sections (vectors, matrices, transforms, frames, projections, homogeneous unification, eigen-SVD-PCA analysis, metrics, curves, spatial structures, queries, numerical survival), reading the bidirectional render-inverse interaction architecture with cross-section dependency awareness.
2. Diagnose broken geometry through the symptom-to-cause index, pipeline bisection, numerical-versus-logical separation, convention-conflict detection, and mathematical code-review checklists.
3. Inform design decisions through integrated projection-choice, metric-encoding coherence, complexity-budgeting, perception-accessibility, and mathematics-documentation frameworks.
4. Demonstrate command through capstone systems (3D exploration pipelines, dimensionality-reduction explorers, geometry-rich 2D displays, debugging audits) evaluated on correctness-robustness-interaction-documentation-justification rubrics.
5. And extend forward through differential-topological-optimization geometry, graphics frontiers, randomized-tensor-manifold data science, and the derive-don't-copy craft identity, consolidating every section's anti-pattern library into the final detection-and-correction discipline.
