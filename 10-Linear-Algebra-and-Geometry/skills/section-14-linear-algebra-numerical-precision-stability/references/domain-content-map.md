1. Engineer numerical reality into visualization mathematics through floating-point literacy (IEEE-754 structure with epsilon scales, representation-error landscapes including geographic magnitude growth, cancellation-accumulation-ordering loss mechanisms, transform-chain and deep-zoom exposures).
2. Apply the conditioning-stability framework (condition-number diagnostics from singular-value ratios, stable algorithm selection with backward-stability reading, absolute-relative tolerance budgets, regularization remedies, determinism engineering across platforms and GPUs).
3. Manage performance economics (operation-cost scaling with batching and bandwidth awareness, BLAS-library leverage and expression optimization, GPU shader mathematics with precision qualifications, data-layout and quantization strategies, LOD-approximation-progressive hierarchies), debug numerically (symptom maps for jitter-flicker-explosion-divergence, residual-magnitude-higher-precision-perturbation instrumentation, tolerance-aware and property-based testing with cross-platform suites, recentering-promotion-swap-normalization remediation playbook), and avoid float-equality, large-coordinate, normal-equations, summation-drift, epsilon-rigidity, GPU-precision, determinism, and premature-optimization failures.
