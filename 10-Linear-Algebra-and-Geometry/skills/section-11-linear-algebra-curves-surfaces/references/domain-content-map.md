## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Command smooth geometry through parametric foundations (curve-as-vector-function, tangent-curvature diagnostics, flattening tolerance, surface extensions with normals and isolines), master Bézier curves (quadratic-cubic control geometry, de Casteljau evaluation and subdivision, Bernstein basis with convex-hull and variation properties, G1 join craft with circle-approximation constants, degree elevation, rational awareness).
2. Operate spline systems (interpolation-approximation tradeoff, cubic spline tridiagonal construction, B-spline local control mechanics, smoothing-penalty regularization, D3 curve vocabulary mapping with monotone honesty, Hermite-Catmull-Rom conversions), solve fitting inverse problems (Bézier least-squares with chord-length parameterization, polyline reconstruction with corner splitting, circle-ellipse fitting, surface and RBF awareness, residual diagnostics).
3. Integrate with rendering and animation (stroke offset and join mathematics, dash arclength behavior, path-following animation via arclength interfaces, morphing compatibility, tessellation, data-curve semantic honesty), and avoid faceted rendering, false oscillation, kink violations, arclength blindness, offset artifacts, parameterization arbitrariness, and smoothing dishonesty failures.
