## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Explore geographic structure through spatial foundations (data-model inventory, CRS-coordinate sanity with lon-lat traps and impossible-location audits, spatial-join and geocode quality, MAUP-ecological-scale framing), craft distribution maps (normalization-first choropleths with denominator reasoning, class-break-method and scheme selections with accessibility obligations, cluster-gap-gradient diagnostics with small-area caution, dot-cartogram-dasymetric-isoline alternatives), investigate point patterns (KDE intensity with bandwidth tradeoffs, hot-spot and exposure-adjusted surfaces, autocorrelation and nearest-neighbor intuitions, grid-binning and hierarchical-zoom MAUP demonstrations, precision-coverage-duplicate quality overlays), compose multivariate spatial views (bivariate choropleths with transparency, layer stacking and small multiples, region typology and stratum comparisons, space-time animation caveats with alternatives, boundary-discontinuity examination).
2. Run integrated protocols (staged investigation sequences, spatial metadata registers, robustness verification.
3. Map-honesty communication prep), and avoid population-masquerade, CRS confusion, MAUP blindness, classification cherry-picking, small-area alarms, geocode ignorance, animation blindness, and ecological leaps failures.
