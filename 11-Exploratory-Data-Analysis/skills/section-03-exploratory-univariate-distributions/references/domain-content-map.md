# Domain Content Map — Univariate Distributions: Reading the Shape of Single Variables

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Read single-variable distributions through histogram craft (binning mathematics with Sturges-Scott-Freedman-Diaconis rules, edge-phase sensitivity, multi-bin inspection discipline, modality-skew-tail-gap vocabulary, normalization and axis honesty, limitation awareness), deploy smooth alternatives (KDE mechanics with bandwidth tradeoffs and boundary artifacts, ECDF and quantile views, Q-Q plots against theoretical and two-sample comparisons, violin-ridgeline families).
2. Recognize distribution families (normal caveats, lognormal-exponential-power-law-binomial signatures, mixture decomposition protocols, transformation anticipation, domain-reference and drift comparisons), handle discrete-categorical univariates (sorted frequency bars, dot-strip-stem-leaf exact views, proportion cautions, cardinality-concentration diagnostics).
3. Operate the systematic sweep (numeric and categorical protocols, batch automation with triage reading, finding documentation feeding the question cycle), and avoid single-bin, conflation, bandwidth-blindness, normal-autopilot, bimodality-dismissal, small-denominator, and undocumented-sweep failures.
