# Domain Content Map — From Exploration to Evidence: Documentation, Reproducibility, and the Communication Handoff

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Convert exploration into evidence through reproducibility foundations (rerun standards with notebook-execution hygiene, data versioning with raw immutability and lineage, seed-approximation determinism control, clean-rerun verification), document findings systematically (observation-context-evidence-implication statements, evidence attachment with caveats, exploration logs valuing dead ends, decision records for transformations and methods, graded finding registers).
2. Execute the communication handoff (audience-shift recognition, finding-driven view curation and polish, narrative construction with uncertainty integration, multi-format artifacts from reports through dashboards, comprehension-verification), enforce rigor and ethics (multiple-comparisons and forking-paths awareness with confirmatory retesting, cherry-picking defenses with sweep documentation, privacy-subgroup-consequence ethics, bias self-checks with peer review, calibrated uncertainty language).
3. Integrate workflow systems (end-to-end pipelines with templates and checklists, collaboration via version control and review workflows, documentation-tooling ecosystems, analysis QA and retrospectives), and avoid notebook chaos, undocumented mutation, memory-only findings, fishing reports, cherry-picked evidence, jargon handoff, seed gaps, and ethics blindness failures.
