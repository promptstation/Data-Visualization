## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Conduct interactive exploration through mantra operationalization (overview-zoom-filter-detail navigation, visual-analytics reasoning loops with serendipity engineering, cost-benefit and tool-context reading).
2. Build brushing-linking systems (brush types and scope decisions, coordinated-view architecture with shared selection state, conditional-distribution-outlier-tracing-cluster-characterization protocols, highlight-filter semantics with quality factors and failure modes), deploy dynamic queries (continuous-control filtering traditions, range-checkbox-facet design decisions with reset safety, faceted-search integration, aggregation-aware semantics, incremental-indexed performance).
3. Manage focus-context navigation (overview-detail patterns, drill-roll-up with breadcrumbs, bookmark-diff-tour modes, disorientation prevention).
4. Integrate practice workflows (session design, finding capture and crystallization, collaboration sharing, accessibility obligations, tooling selection matrices), and avoid purposeless-interaction, latency-tolerance, linking-overload, state-invisibility, dead-ends, unshareable-discovery, inaccessibility, and tool-fanaticism failures.
