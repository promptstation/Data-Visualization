## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the philosophical and practical foundations of EDA as the investigative heart of the visualization discipline: the exploratory versus confirmatory distinction — the stance that defines the practice: the Tukey formulation (the EDA tradition from the 1977 canon: the attitude of letting data suggest questions rather than testing predetermined hypotheses, the "strong versus weak" box distinction separating confirmatory inference from exploratory discovery); the hypothesis-generating role (the exploration-before-modeling sequence: the why-fitting-models-to-unexamined-data-produces-confident-nonsense insight, the discovery-versus-verification cycle where EDA feeds formal analysis rather than replacing it); the EDA mindset (the curiosity-and-skepticism balance: the belief-that-patterns-exist combined with distrust-of-first-impressions, the play-and-serendipity element: the undirected-looking that produces unexpected findings, the detective framing: the data-as-crime-scene metaphor with the analyst collecting evidence before theorizing); and the modern relevance (the big-data-era necessity: the larger-and-messier-datasets-increasing-the-exploration-burden argument, the self-service-analytics context: the EDA skills for non-statisticians using BI tools, the AI-era positioning: the human-judgment layer that automated pipelines cannot replace) as the stance foundation; the visualization-first principle — why EDA is a graphics discipline: the look-before-you-compute doctrine (the Anscombe-quartet lesson as the canonical demonstration: the four datasets with identical summary statistics and completely different structures revealed only by plotting, the summary-statistics-as-lossy-compression reading motivating visual inspection); the visual-system advantages (the preattentive pattern detection from the perception courses: the human-visual-system-as-outlier-and-cluster-detector argument, the parallel processing of graphical displays versus serial reading of tables, the shape-of-data gestalt: the distribution, gap, and trend perception that resists numeric summarization); the graphic-versus-numeric complementarity (the when-numbers-win contexts: the precise-value lookup and small-comparison tasks, the when-graphics-win contexts: the structure, anomaly, and relationship discovery, the paired-presentation discipline: the graphic-with-annotated-statistics standard); and the EDA-specific chart literacy (the diagnostic-chart vocabulary differing from presentation charts: the density-over-decor priorities, the multi-view convention where exploration uses many rough views rather than one polished display) as the visual foundation; the EDA workflow cycle — the process structure: the question-view-inspect-refine loop (the iterative cycle: the initial question motivating a view, the view revealing features prompting inspection, the inspection generating refined questions and new views, the cycle-until-diminishing-returns termination judgment); the systematic-coverage principle (the univariate-then-bivariate-then-multivariate progression: the structured exploration order preventing cherry-picked wandering, the data-shape-driven agenda: the categorical-numeric-temporal-spatial inventory determining which explorations apply, the checklist-versus-intuition balance: the systematic-sweep-then-free-investigation two-phase approach); the documentation-throughout habit (the notes-and-annotations-as-first-class-outputs principle: the recording-questions-surprises-and-decisions discipline previewing section 14's reproducibility systems, the analysis-log traditions from the reproducible-research literature); and the session design (the time-boxed-exploration-sprint patterns: the focused-question sessions versus open-ended familiarization, the toolchain readiness: the notebook-environment-and-data-access prerequisites from the modern ecosystem) as the process layer; the data-analysis landscape position — where EDA sits: the pipeline integration (the acquisition-cleaning-exploration-modeling-communication sequence: the EDA phase informing every downstream decision, the cleaning-exploration interleaving reality: the quality discoveries during exploration feeding back into preparation from section 2's profiling systems); the role differentiation (the analyst-versus-statistician-versus-engineer EDA emphases: the domain-question-driven versus method-driven versus pipeline-driven exploration styles, the visualization-specialist advantage: the graphics-fluency enabling richer exploratory views than statistics-first traditions typically produce); the organizational contexts (the business-analytics EDA: the KPI investigation and performance diagnosis workflows, the research-and-journalism EDA: the story-finding investigations from the data-journalism traditions, the product-analytics EDA: the user-behavior pattern discovery contexts); and the discipline boundaries (the EDA versus data-mining distinction: the human-in-the-loop visual investigation versus automated pattern extraction, the visual-analytics framing: the analytical-reasoning-supported-by-visual-interfaces definition from the research canon, the exploratory-statistics overlap: the graphical-methods traditions in statistics departments) as the positioning layer; the modern EDA ecosystem — the tooling landscape: the notebook environments (the Jupyter-and-Observable traditions: the interactive-computation-with-inline-graphics workflow, the literate-programming heritage: the code-narrative-visualization interweaving from the reproducible-research literature); the programming ecosystems (the pandas-and-R-tidyverse lineage: the data-manipulation-grammar enabling fluent exploration, the plotting-layer options from static libraries through interactive frameworks connecting to the broader visualization toolchain); the automated-profiling tools (the library-generated overview reports as exploration accelerators: the starting-point-not-substitute positioning from section 2's profiling discipline); the BI-platform exploration (the drag-and-drop investigation in Tableau-and-Power-BI-class tools: the speed-versus-flexibility tradeoffs against code-based EDA); and the emerging-assistant landscape (the AI-copilot exploration support at awareness: the generated-code-and-suggested-views capabilities with the verification-obligation caveats) as the ecosystem survey; the foundational anti-patterns — the failure library: the confirmatory-masquerade arriving with conclusions and cherry-picking supporting slices, remedied by the honest-exploration stance and documentation discipline; the summary-first-blindness trusting means and correlations before distributions, remedied by the look-before-you-compute doctrine; the tool-driven-exploration letting dashboard defaults pick the questions, remedied by the data-shape-driven agenda; the exploration-without-notes losing discoveries to memory, remedied by the documentation-throughout habit; the eternal-exploration never converging to decisions, remedied by the diminishing-returns termination judgment; and the visualization-contempt dismissing graphics as unrigorous decoration, remedied by the Anscombe-quartet evidence and the complementary-pairing discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.
