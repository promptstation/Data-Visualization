# Domain Content Map — Bivariate Relationships: Scatter, Correlation, and Two-Variable Structure

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Investigate bivariate structure through scatterplot mastery (axis-assignment and aspect-ratio craft, overplotting remedy families from alpha-jitter through hexbin-density-sampling, form-direction-strength-cluster-fan-outlier reading vocabulary, marginal-reference-annotation enhancements), quantify with the correlation family (Pearson mechanics with geometric cosine reading and testing caveats, Spearman-Kendall rank alternatives with selection guidance, causation-range-Simpson-outlier-nonlinearity pitfall discipline, matrix-correlogram displays), pursue nonlinearity (Anscombe operationalization, LOESS-spline smoothing with honesty caveats and hypothesis framing, log-log-semilog linearization with labeling obligations, functional-form vocabulary, mutual-information awareness), handle mixed types (grouped boxplot-violin-strip comparisons, contingency-mosaic-parallel-sets displays, conditional facet interaction hunting, paired Bland-Altman conventions).
2. Operate systematic pairing workflows (exhaustive-hypothesis-target pair selection, relationship inventories, univariate-context iteration, pairplot automation), and avoid correlation-without-scatter, overplotting acceptance, Pearson misuse, leverage ignorance, range blindness, mosaic confusion, smoothing overfit, and cherry-picking failures.
