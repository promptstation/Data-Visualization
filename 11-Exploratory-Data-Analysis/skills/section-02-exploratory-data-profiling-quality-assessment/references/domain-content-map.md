# Domain Content Map — Data Profiling and Quality Assessment: The First-Contact Protocol

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Execute systematic first-contact profiling through the structured protocol (shape-size-type inventory, row-inspection rituals, column sweeps, schema-declared-versus-actual archaeology, format recognition, suspicion-default mindset with re-profiling habits), investigate missingness (rate quantification with null-type detection, matrix-pattern visualization with block-versus-scattered reading, MCAR-MAR-MNAR mechanism reasoning with subgroup diagnostics, keep-and-visualize transparency).
2. Audit value quality (type impostors, sentinel hunting, distribution plausibility with digit-preference detection, uniqueness-consistency rules, quality-dimension frameworks), wield the toolchain (programmatic sweeps, automated report generators with reading discipline, validation frameworks, notebook integration, report artifacts), govern the cleaning boundary (raw-preservation discipline, blocking-annotating triage, scope decisions with filter documentation, caveat communication), and avoid analysis-first, missingness-ignoring, sentinel-swallowing, silent-cleaning, report-passivity, and one-time-profiling failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the systematic first-contact investigation that precedes all analysis: the profiling concept — knowing the data before using it: the first-contact protocol (the structured initial sweep: the shape-size-types-columns inventory, the head-tail-and-random-sample inspection ritual: the looking-at-actual-rows discipline that catches problems no summary reveals, the column-by-column profiling pass: the per-field distribution-missingness-range-value-counts review); the schema investigation (the declared-versus-actual schema comparison: the type-drift detection where columns hold unexpected content, the metadata archaeology: the data-dictionary-and-lineage-document hunting, the semantic verification: the confirming-what-columns-actually-mean through value inspection and source consultation); the size-and-shape awareness (the row-and-column-count context for tool and method selection connecting to section 13's scale strategies, the wide-versus-long format recognition: the reshape requirements preview from section 12's transformation systems, the table-relationship inventory: the joins-and-foreign-keys mapping for multi-table data); and the profiling-mindset (the suspicion-as-default posture: the assume-problems-exist-until-proven-clean discipline, the pattern-recognition targets: the sentinel values, truncated strings, mixed formats, and impossible values as the standard suspects, the profiling-as-ongoing habit: the re-profiling-after-every-transformation principle) as the contact foundation; the missing-data investigation — the absence analysis: the missingness quantification (the per-column-missing-rate computation: the null-NaN-empty-string-and-sentinel distinction requiring explicit detection logic, the overall-completeness summary versus column-level detail: the granularity discipline); the missingness-pattern visualization (the missingness-matrix displays: the rows-by-columns presence-absence heatmaps revealing structured gaps, the pattern-clustering reading: the block-missingness indicating systematic collection failures versus scattered-missingness suggesting random events, the row-wise-completeness histograms: the record-quality distribution views); the missingness-mechanism reasoning (the MCAR-MAR-MNAR taxonomy at practitioner depth: the missing-completely-at-random versus at-random versus not-at-random mechanism distinctions from the statistical tradition, the mechanism-investigation methods: the comparing-missing-versus-present-subgroups diagnostics, the MNAR-signal awareness: the missingness-itself-as-data reading where absence carries meaning — the non-responses, sensor-failures, and refused-answers cases); and the handling-decision preview (the drop-impute-flag-indicator strategy families with their exploration-phase biases, the keep-and-visualize-missingness discipline for EDA: the transparency-over-silent-imputation principle detailed in section 12) as the absence layer; the value-quality auditing — the content investigation: the type-impostor detection (the numeric-columns-holding-text scanning: the coercion-failure patterns in parsing, the string-format inconsistencies: the date-format-variations, case-variations, and whitespace contamination audits, the encoding artifacts: the mojibake-and-escape-sequence detection from the data-ingestion traditions); the sentinel-and-magic-value hunting (the impossible-value scanning: the negative-ages, future-dates, and out-of-range-measurements, the conventional-sentinel inventory: the -999, 9999, and unknown-string placeholders masquerading as data, the duplicate-representation detection: the same-entity-as-multiple-values from inconsistent entry); the distribution-plausibility checks (the per-column histograms-and-descriptives against domain expectations: the physical-limits-and-business-rules verification, the digit-preference and rounding-pattern detection: the too-round-values heuristic for fabricated-or-bucketed data, the spike-and-gap identification previewing section 3's distribution reading); the uniqueness-and-consistency audits (the duplicate-row detection with exact-and-fuzzy-match distinctions, the cross-column consistency rules: the start-before-end-and-part-less-than-whole validations, the referential-integrity checks across joined tables); and the quality-dimension framework (the validity-completeness-uniqueness-timeliness-accuracy-consistency dimension vocabulary from the data-quality literature: the structured assessment-report organization) as the content layer; the profiling toolchain — the acceleration instruments: the programmatic profiling (the pandas-describe-and-value-counts foundations: the numeric-versus-categorical summary behaviors, the information-and-memory-usage inspection methods, the custom-profiling-function patterns: the per-column-type sweep automation); the automated-report generators (the ydata-profiling-lineage tools producing comprehensive HTML overviews: the correlation-missingness-distribution panels as exploration starting points, the report-reading discipline: the automated-flags-requiring-human-investigation principle, the large-dataset-performance-considerations and sampling options); the validation frameworks (the expectation-and-assertion tooling from the Great-Expectations tradition: the declarative-data-quality-rules for ongoing verification, the schema-validation libraries: the type-and-constraint enforcement at ingestion boundaries); the notebook-integrated profiling (the inline-visualization profiling functions: the quick-look graphics conventions, the progressive-disclosure profiling: the summary-first-detail-on-demand notebook organization); and the profiling-output artifacts (the data-quality-report structure: the findings-severity-recommendations format for team communication, the profiling-notebook-as-deliverable convention connecting to section 14's documentation systems) as the toolchain layer; the cleaning-decision discipline — the preparation boundary: the clean-versus-preserve tension (the exploration-phase principle of preserving raw data: the never-modify-the-source discipline with transformations in derived copies, the cleaning-decision documentation: the every-exclusion-and-imputation-recorded obligation); the issue-triage framework (the blocking-versus-annotating classification: the problems-preventing-analysis versus problems-requiring-awareness distinction, the fix-now-versus-fix-later sequencing by analysis impact, the domain-consultation triggers: the when-to-ask-data-owners decision rules); the subset-and-scope decisions (the analysis-population definition: the inclusion-exclusion-criteria articulation before exploration, the filter-documentation discipline: the reproducible-subset-definitions from section 14's workflow systems); and the quality-communication (the data-caveat annotations carried into all downstream visualizations: the quality-context-obligation from the communication traditions, the fitness-for-purpose statements: the what-this-data-can-and-cannot-support conclusions) as the boundary layer; and the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the analysis-before-profiling rushing to charts on unexamined data, remedied by the first-contact protocol; the missingness-ignoring treating nulls as empty rather than signal, remedied by the pattern-mechanism investigation; the sentinel-swallowing computing means over -999 placeholders, remedied by the magic-value hunting; the silent-cleaning dropping rows without documentation, remedied by the preserve-and-record discipline; the profile-report-passivity accepting automated summaries without inspection, remedied by the human-investigation principle; and the one-time-profiling treating quality as a phase rather than a habit, remedied by the re-profile-after-transformation rule with detection methods as the diagnostic.
