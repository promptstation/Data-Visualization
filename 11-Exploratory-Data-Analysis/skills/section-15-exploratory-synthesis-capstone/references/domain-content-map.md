# Domain Content Map — Synthesis and Capstone: The Complete Investigative Practice

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Synthesize complete investigative practice by executing the end-to-end EDA lifecycle across all fourteen preceding sections with data-shape-driven method selection, iteration-convergence dynamics, and cross-section composition fluency.
2. Audit investigation health through coverage-method-documentation quality reviews, robustness-battery and alternative-explanation finding verification, data-quality interaction awareness, wrong-and-missed-finding triage, and meta-cognitive reflection habits.
3. Demonstrate command through capstone vehicles (complete novel-dataset investigations, shape-specialized deep dives, scale-adapted explorations, exploration-to-communication pipelines.
4. Audit-and-remediation exercises) evaluated on coverage-method-findings-reproducibility-communication-anti-pattern rubrics.
5. And extend forward through statistical-learning-causal-Bayesian methods, AI-real-time-collaborative tool evolution, domain-specialization paths, and the investigator-not-chart-maker craft identity, consolidating every section's anti-pattern library into the final prevention system.
